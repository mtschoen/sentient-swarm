# Agent instructions

## Global constraints (binding)

- Plain JavaScript ES modules and WGSL. No TypeScript. No runtime dependencies. Vite and Playwright are dev dependencies only.
- Shaders are loaded with `fetch(new URL('./shaders/<name>.wgsl', import.meta.url))` so the page also runs from any static server without Vite.
- Full-word identifiers (`maximum`, not `max`; `position`, not `pos`) in JavaScript. WGSL may use `vec2f` style built-in names but user identifiers follow the same rule.
- No em-dashes (U+2014) anywhere, including comments and docs.
- Every source file under about 500 lines, or carrying a one-line note at the top saying why.
- No hard-coded machine paths. No wall-clock assertions in tests; inject time.
- Mobile WebGPU limits are designed for: at most 8 storage buffers bound per shader stage; agent data interleaved in 16-byte aligned structs with no `vec3` in storage layouts; `@workgroup_size` at most 256; workgroup memory at most 16 KB; timestamp queries optional.
- Neighbor contributions are averaged, never summed. Per-agent fine-pass neighbor sampling is capped at `MAXIMUM_NEIGHBOR_SAMPLES = 32`.
- The world is 32 by 18 world units, two views wide and high (`WORLD_WIDTH`, `WORLD_HEIGHT` in `src/sim/world.js`, re-exported by `layout.js` and substituted into `common.wgsl` through `SHADER_CONSTANTS`; the coarse field is the world in 0.25-unit cells, 128 by 72, and `gridDimensions` keeps the neighbor grid within `MAXIMUM_GRID_CELLS` = 65536 by raising the cell above the neighbor radius only where needed, which at this size is `desktopUltra` alone: 0.08 becomes about 0.0938). The view is the largest rectangle of the window's aspect inside a 16 by 9 screen (`viewSizeForAspect`), centered on the follow camera (`src/game/camera.js`, `Viewport.setCamera`, `Viewport.viewBounds`) and mapped to the whole canvas so the game fills the window at any aspect between 1:4 and 4:1 (letterboxed beyond that). The camera follows the player swarm (critically damped, 0.30 s smooth time, a dead zone of 10 percent of the view, clamped so the view may extend past the world's edge by `CAMERA_OVERSHOOT_FRACTION` (0.12) of its extent on that axis, so the border is seen coming and a swarm pressed against an edge sits clear of the screen edge; an axis where the view is as large as the world stays centered; cut on start, quit and respawn, still in watch mode) in solo with the solo zoom, shipped ON and calm (owner ruling 2026-11-14 for on, calmed 2026-11-15 after the strong settings zoomed in too far and flipped too often: `SOLO_ZOOM_DEFAULTS` in `src/game/solo-zoom.js`, punch-in 0.5 and pull-out 1 in ln, so the zoom ranges between 0.5, the whole world, and 1.3 with the crowd, smoothed over 1.2 s at a rate limit of 0.35 ln per second, crowd reference 1, swarm fit fraction 1.25; the lab ranges `SOLO_ZOOM_RANGES` go further): `SoloZoomController`; a punch-in when crowded around the swarm, a pull-out when the action is just outside the view; one live seat only, never under reduced motion (zoom 1 there), capped so the swarm stays in view (`zoomFit`: `0.5 * min(viewWidth, viewHeight) * fitFraction / (swarmRadius + 1)`; the 12-ship swarm's peak radius is 1.234, so 1.25 allows about 2.5 at the 16:9 view), and the bomb radius, spawn safe distance and pickup reach stay unzoomed while the spawn gate ring's and the beam shard placement's view cap their zoom at 1, `Game.ringView`); with two seats it centers on the live seats' focuses and eases its zoom (`Camera.update` `targetZoom`, `CAMERA_ZOOM_SMOOTH_SECONDS` 0.5) between 1 and `MINIMUM_CAMERA_ZOOM` (0.5, the whole world) to fit them (`fitZoom`, `framingCenter`; see Co-op), so the view is `viewSize / zoom` (`Game.view`; `Viewport.setCamera({ centerX, centerY, zoom })`) and `Viewport.visibleBounds` is the world rectangle on screen, the one the edge indicators use; the simulation never reads it. The playable world bounds (walls, clamps, spawns, pickups, projectile edges and the beam's reach: `Game.worldBounds`, `Simulation.setWorldBounds`) are the whole world at every aspect, and the alive target is a fraction of capacity alone, so every aspect plays the same world with the same count. The view (`Game.setView`) sets the spawn gate ring, the minimum spawn distance (a fraction of the view width, `Simulation.setSpawnViewWidth`), the pickup reach and the bomb radius. Enemies seek the nearest live player (one or two seek points) from anywhere in the world. Renderer works in device pixels.
- Species count is 5. Coarse field is 128 by 72 cells (the world in 0.25-unit cells) with 8 float channels (5 species densities, 1 threat, 2 padding). Shipped shares are 0.9 Chaser, 0.03 Orbiter, 0.02 Skittish, 0 Lattice, 0.05 Predator (`DEFAULT_SHARES`, owner ruling R38); the lab's `equalFive` mix restores equal shares.
- Commit after every task with a message in the imperative.

## Player swarm

The player is a swarm of ships, not a single ship; `Game.seats` holds one `PlayerSeat` (`src/game/player-seat.js`) per player, and this section describes one seat (the Co-op section covers two); where the sections below still describe the single ship, its hit latch, lives or respawn, this section wins.

- The player-controlled point (`PlayerShip`) moves as the ship did and cannot be hit. `PlayerSwarm` (`src/game/player-swarm.js`, CPU) flies 12 small white ships (`INITIAL_SWARM_SHIPS`, `?swarm=N` overrides; drawn and hit at `SWARM_SHIP_SIZE`) up to 256 (`MAXIMUM_SWARM_SHIPS` in `src/sim/layout.js`) around it, one more per 100 scored kills (`KILLS_PER_SWARM_SHIP`); award lives are dropped, bombs stay. Each ship is its own agent flocking by the enemy Chaser's steering rules ported to the CPU (`src/game/swarm-flocking.js`: seek toward its slot group's roaming waypoint near the point, separation, cohesion and pressure from its neighbours, an acceleration cap), plus a per-slot temperament from `slotHash`, wander, a leash to the point and attack runs along the aim while firing (the attack bias swings along and against the aim on a `SWARM_ATTACK_SECONDS` cycle whose phase is evenly spaced over the slots, `attackPhaseFor`, so a steady share of the swarm faces the aim at any moment instead of the ships running in lockstep). A ship flies along its course (its velocity's direction) and points along its heading: while firing, `faceStep` (`src/game/swarm-facing.js`) turns the heading off the course toward the aim by up to `SWARM_FACING_LIMIT_RADIANS` (180 degrees less the fire cone, 145), squeezing the courses into the fire cone in proportion (`facingFor`) and turning at most `SWARM_FACING_TURN_RADIANS_PER_SECOND` (`SWARM_FACING_RETURN_RADIANS_PER_SECOND`, five revolutions a second, while the heading is outside the fire cone, so a swing of the aim does not silence the whole swarm), so ships flying across or straight away from the aim still shoot at it (a lower limit shuts out the courses nearest to straight away); not firing, the heading returns to the course. `SwarmFire` (`src/game/swarm-fire.js`) fires each shot as ONE ship firing ONE projectile (scatter: 1, 2, 3 pellets by tier) from its own position along its own heading, only within 35 degrees of the aim and after the ship's own cooldown. What a shot is comes from `swarmShotRecipe(kind, tier)` (`src/game/swarm-shots.js`: radius, lifetime, jitter, fan, homing); how often comes from the rate law in `src/game/swarm-fire-rate.js`: `aggregateShotsPerSecond = baseShotsPerSecond * (shipCount / 12) ^ exponent`, each ship's interval being the ship count over that aggregate times a rate-neutral 0.8 to 1.2 per-slot jitter, with cooldowns carrying their remainder (base 65 per second at 12 ships and about 220 at 256, exponent 0.4 for base and scatter; pulse and sidewinders a fixed aggregate by tier across ships in rotation). `WeaponState` still owns the armed kind, tier, charge, expiry and the `weaponFire` cue cadence, but its volley spawns are not queued by the game; the rail turrets are gunner ships (see Rail gunners below) and the beam fires from the centroid. Randomness is the `random` injected into `SwarmFire`.
- Contact: `Simulation.setSwarmShips(ships, radius, { seat, invulnerable, neutralImmune })` stores one slot table per seat (`Simulation.swarmSeats`) and the step uploads them to one contact uniform of `MAXIMUM_CONTACT_SLOTS` (512) slots, seat k's slot s being slot `k * MAXIMUM_SWARM_SHIPS + s`, with an `armedSeats` bit per seat that is not invulnerable or neutral-immune and a `projectileArmedSeats` word (the first padding word; read by `versus_hits.wgsl` in place of `armedSeats`) with a bit per seat that is not invulnerable, so the two masks are equal unless a seat is neutral-immune; `player_collide.wgsl` runs one workgroup per slot of `seatCount` seats, walks the grid around its ship and sets the slot's bit in the persistent 8-word `swarmHitMask` stats words (seat 2's slots in `secondSwarmHitMask`, words 32 to 39); `PlayerSwarm.applyHits` guards stale and reused slots by each ship's first uploaded step. The GPU hit latch and `playerHitSequence` are unused. Losing the last ship of the last seat still in the run is the run's one death (`RunState` with one life): death clear, then game over. The HUD lives readout shows the swarm size; `src/render/swarm.js` draws the swarm, each ship and the point extrapolated by the same alpha as `Camera.sample` (`PlayerSwarm.shipsAt`, `blendedPoint` in `src/game/swarm-blend.js`), so they cannot judder against the follow camera and stamp that into the trails. `tests/browser/swarm-spike.mjs` is the browser evidence run (not part of `check:gpu`); `tests/browser/player.mjs` (in `check:gpu`) checks the swarm contact pass and hit mask, kill-all and the pickup blast.
- **Fire log (diagnostic)**: `?debug=fire` shows on screen why the swarm went silent while fire was held, for a device with no console. `FireLog` (`src/game/fire-log.js`, pure, sampled once per `Game.update` through `Game.setFireLog`) keeps the newest releases of the fire input, each with the touch event that came last (`lastEvent` of `src/input/touch.js`: start, end, cancel, blur, ignored), the newest thin stretches (quarter-second windows of held fire with under half the ship shots the armed weapon's law gives, for fire that sputters without stopping; the status line counts them against all judged windows) and the newest silences (fire held while Playing with no ship shot for longer than the armed weapon explains), each with the reason from `SwarmFire.report` (ships, in the cone, off cooldown, fired, the law's rate), the aim and the movement; `src/ui/fire-debug.js` draws its lines under the HUD (`.fire-debug` in `hud.css`, never takes a touch) and `window.__murmuration.fireLog` exposes it.
- **Bee motion** (`src/game/swarm-motion.js`, fixed constants): the ships hover about the point like bees. `SWARM_TIGHTNESS` (2) adds a harmonic well toward the point (desired speed `TIGHTNESS_WELL_RATE * SWARM_TIGHTNESS * distance`, capped at the ship's maximum speed) and shrinks the roaming reach by `SWARM_ROAM_SCALE` = `1 / (1 + SWARM_TIGHTNESS)`; `SWARM_BUZZ` (1.5) adds a per-slot random velocity of `SWARM_BUZZ * BUZZ_SPEED_SHARE * cruise speed` (`buzzDirection`, redrawn 3 to 7 times a second) to the ship's movement directly (as a steering input the steering response and the acceleration cap smoothed it away); `SWARM_FOLLOW` (1) is the share of the point's velocity (`PlayerSwarm`, smoothed over `FOLLOW_SMOOTH_SECONDS`, capped at `SWARM_MAXIMUM_SPEED`) that carries the flock: `flockStep` moves every ship by that carry before steering, a ship's `velocityX` and `velocityY` are its velocity in the carried frame and its course is the direction of the whole movement, so the flock behaves around a moving point as around a still one.
- **Rail gunners** (`src/game/swarm-gunners.js`, `src/game/gunner-fire.js`): each rail turret is a real ship of the swarm. `PlayerSwarm.setGunnerCount(weapons.railTurrets)` (called by `PlayerSeat.flock` every step, so `dropAll`, a down seat, death, quit and revive clear the marks) marks up to that many live ships as gunners (`assignGunners`: the nearest non-gunner ship to each free station, a dead gunner's station passes to the next nearest on the next update; the count is clamped to the live ships, so a swarm smaller than its turrets makes every ship a gunner). Station k sits at the point plus `stationRadius` (`homeRadiusFor(alive) * GUNNER_STATION_REACH` = 0.8) along the aim angle plus `railTurretOffsets(count)[k]`, so the ring rotates with the aim (the last nonzero aim when there is none). `flockStep` replaces a gunner's roaming waypoint with its station (`GUNNER_SEEK` 1.6), drops the well, cohesion, pressure and attack bias, and scales wander by `GUNNER_WANDER_SCALE` (0.25), buzz by `GUNNER_BUZZ_SCALE` (0.3) and the speed floor by `GUNNER_SPEED_FLOOR_SCALE` (0.1) so it holds station; `faceStep` sets its heading to the station angle every step. `GunnerFire` fires one volley per gunner per rail interval (0.3 s, `railTier` picks the needle count, spacing and lifetime through `gunnerShotRecipe`) from the gunner's own position along its heading, while fire is held with a nonzero aim, beside any weapon including the beam; `SwarmFire.distribute` returns them as `turretSpawns` and takes `railTier` in its control. Gunners are ordinary ships in the contact pass and can be hit. Gunners never fire the armed weapon: the rate law's aggregate is still that of all the ships (so rail pickups do not cut the main weapon's firepower) but `SwarmFire.fireShips` spreads it over the non-gunner ships only, and with every ship a gunner the armed weapon is quiet (`SwarmFire.report` counts non-gunners in the cone and off cooldown, and expects the law's aggregate of all ships capped at one shot per non-gunner per step, none for an all-gunner swarm, which `FireLog` does not log as silent). The beam leaves the centroid of the non-gunner ships (the controlled point when there are none; `distribute(fire, ships, point, control)`); the camera focus still uses the whole-swarm centroid. The ring is drawn in a 0.22 quad (0.17 ring radius), other ships keep their 0.16 quad. `WeaponState` keeps only `railTurrets` and `railTier` (the HUD `RAIL xN` readout). `src/render/swarm.js` draws a thin rail-hue ring around each gunner.

## Co-op (stage 1)

Two players share one screen and one score against the neutral swarm; solo is one seat and plays exactly as before (every co-op call with one seat is the solo call).

- **Seats**: `Game.seats` holds one or two `PlayerSeat`s (`MAXIMUM_PLAYER_SEATS` = 2, `src/sim/layout.js`); `Game.setPlayerCount(1 | 2)` works in Ready only, and `quitToTitle` and `setWatch` return the game to one seat (`removeSecondSeat`). A seat owns its `PlayerShip`, `PlayerSwarm`, `SwarmFire`, `WeaponState`, held fire and bomb levels, `down` flag and revive immunity; `Game.player`, `swarm`, `swarmFire`, `weapons`, `swarmControls`, `firingHeld` and `bombHeld` are the local seat's (`Game.localSeat`, index `Game.localSeatIndex`: seat 1 except on the online guest, see Online). Each seat has its own fire rate law (`SwarmFire` per seat). Two seats start `SEAT_START_OFFSET` (1.5) either side of the bounds center on x (`seatStarts`). `src/game/seats.js` holds the helpers over the seat list (`liveSeats`, `seekTargets`, `pickupAnchor`, `partnerFor`, `framedFocuses`) and the game-side seat work that takes the `Game` (`uploadSwarms`, `fireSeats`, `applySwarmHits`, `reviveSeats`); a seat is live while it is not down and has ships.
- **Joining**: only at the title, by pad. `SeatJoin` (`src/input/seat-join.js`, pure, polled by `createCoopJoin` in `src/ui/coop-join.js` while the title shows) seats a pad on a fresh press: on a pad that is not the first connected one any of standard buttons 0 to 7 (`SECOND_PAD_JOIN_BUTTON_INDICES`), on the first connected pad only a shoulder or trigger, buttons 4 to 7 (`JOIN_BUTTON_INDICES`), since its face buttons start the solo run. A shoulder or trigger press on seat 2's pad leaves. Buttons held when the title opens, or at the first poll, count as held; a browser lists a pad only after its first press, so an unseated pad that first appears with a join button down joins at once, while seat 2's own pad appearing is primed (no leave press). The press that joins is not also a menu press (`menuInput.setExtraPad` re-reads the held buttons). A toast says `Player 2 joined` or `Player 2 left`; `createJoinBanner` (`src/ui/join-banner.js`, `.join-banner` in `menu.css`, in seat 2's color, out of flow at the top of the title menu) shows how to join or that seat 2 joined. Starting calls `Game.setPlayerCount(coopJoin.playerCount)`; there is no mid-run joining. The lab is solo: `openLab` resets the join. `createPadStatus` (`src/ui/pad-status.js`, driven by `createCoopJoin.update` from the raw `navigator.getGamepads()` list) keeps two rows in the bottom left of the title, always laid out while it shows (fixed height, `pointer-events: none`, `.pad-status` in `menu.css`), in the seats' colors: P1 the pad seat 1 reads (the first listed pad that is not seat 2's) and P2 seat 2's pad when joined, else whether a second pad is detected; each shows the pad's id cut to `GAMEPAD_ID_MAXIMUM_LENGTH` (40, shared with the connect toast through `shortPadId`), a non-standard `mapping` warning, and a dot that brightens while any button on the pad is pressed; with no pad P1 says keyboard and mouse are in use and that a browser lists a pad only after a button press. It is evidence for pads the page cannot see or reads with a non-standard mapping; the banner stays the invite.
- **Devices** (couch; online the local seat reads `input.state` at its own index and the remote seat reads nothing, see Online): seat 1 reads keyboard, mouse, touch and the first connected pad that is not seat 2's (`readGamepad` with `excludeIndex`, `input.state`); seat 2 reads its one pad by `Gamepad.index` (`readGamepadAt`, `input.seatTwoState`), so no pad drives both seats. Menus read the first pad and, once joined, seat 2's. The first gesture of either seat starts or restarts the run. Seat 2's pad disconnecting while the title is open unseats seat 2 (the banner returns to its invite, no toast, no pause); mid-run it pauses under the pause menu with the toast `Player 2 controller disconnected`, and `coopJoin.handleConnect` gives the seat to that pad returning under any index, the same one included (toast `Player 2 controller reconnected`; seating re-primes the menu input so a waking button press is not a confirm). Rumble goes to the pad of the seat concerned: `withHaptics` routes bomb (the bomb effect carries the seat index with two seats), pickup and seat down through `padForSeat`, and `createLifeWatcher` routes the extra-life rumble to seat 1's pad; `rumble` takes `undefined` for the first pad with an actuator (solo), an index, or `null` for none.
- **Down and revive**: a seat that loses its last ship while another still has ships goes down (`seatGoesDown`): it drops its weapons and turrets (its gunner marks clear with the count), the multiplier resets (`score.recordHit`, the solo death rule), a strength 1 bloom shockwave plays at its last ship, its pad rumbles, and a running time warp or beam time is left alone. A down seat does not move, fire, collect or bomb, is not drawn and is not framed. `SeatRevival` (`src/game/seat-revival.js`) counts scored kills for each down seat; at `REVIVE_KILLS` (100) the seat returns with `REVIVE_SHIPS` (12) ships at `REVIVE_OFFSET` (0.6) beside the first live seat's point (`revivePoint`: the other side at a wall, inside the world bounds), with `REVIVE_INVULNERABLE_SECONDS` (2) of contact immunity for that seat alone and a 0.5 shockwave; a seat revived while beam time runs fires the beam for the remainder (`joinBeamTime`), and a seat that is down when beam time starts is skipped, and one down when it ends holds no override (its stash was dropped when it went down). When no seat is left live the run's one death follows (death clear, then game over); two seats wiped in one stats frame are that death, not a down and a revive, and the death flood is at the last-wiped seat's point (seat 2's when both fell in one frame).
- **Shared score, bombs and awards**: one `ScoreKeeper`, bomb pool and `Awards`; every scored kill grows the swarm of every seat that is not down (`PlayerSwarm.addKills`; a down seat gets no growth, and `setInitialSwarmShips` skips it). The bomb press of either seat that is not down spends the pool, centered on that seat's swarm with the unzoomed view's radius (`bombRadiusForView`), so zooming out never makes a bomb a world clear.
- **Pickups**: one pickup rotation of five (scatter, rail, pulse, sidewinders, time warp; the beam is not in it); the spawn anchor is the midpoint of the live seats' points (`pickupAnchor`) and the base-gun recovery guard waits for every live seat to be on the base gun. The seat whose point or ship reaches a pickup collects it (its own `WeaponState` and turrets); a time warp stays global. Beam shards are shared: one count (`BeamShards`), collected by whichever seat not down reaches one, and the completing shard starts beam time for every seat not down (`startBeamTime`; each seat's `WeaponState.beginBeamTime`, its own loadout stashed and restored at the end). At most one `weaponFire` cue plays per update, seat 1 first.
- **Neutral swarm**: `Simulation.setPlayerState` carries the first live seat's point (seat 1's when none is live) and `setSecondPlayer` and `clearSecondPlayer` the second live seat's, in `SimParams` bytes 68 to 79 (the active flag at 68, the position at 72); `steer.wgsl` seeks the nearer of the two, `spawn.wgsl` keeps spawns `nearestPlayerDistance` from both, and `SpawnDirector` takes the second live seat as `partner` (the safe radius and the aim lane apply to either player). Each seat's beam has its own projectile slot (`Projectiles.setBeamSlotCount`, `setBeam(beam, seat)`). Seek has an optional range, lab only: `seekRange` (`SimParams` byte 256, f32 world units, 0 unlimited and the default, which makes `seekRangeFactor` in `steer.wgsl` exactly 1; `Simulation.setSeekRange`, 0 to `MAXIMUM_SEEK_RANGE` 40, carried across a capacity swap; the `seekRange` lab knob and mix field, restored on leaving the lab) multiplies every species' seek by `1 - smoothstep(0.6 * range, range, distance)` to the nearest live seek point (`nearestPlayerDistance`, so either seat's), so beyond the range enemies only flock and drift; the game never sets it, so shipped play is unchanged (`tests/game/seek-range-guard.test.js` guards this). The `SimParams` uniform is `SIM_PARAMS_BYTES` = 272 bytes; `steer-check.js` scenario (h) checks the fade for one and two seek points.
- **Camera**: with two live seats the camera centers on the bounding box of their camera focuses (`framingCenter`) and targets `fitZoom`: `min(1, viewWidth / spanX, viewHeight / spanY)` over the span of the focuses plus `CAMERA_FIT_MARGIN` (2.5) world units on each side, never below `MINIMUM_CAMERA_ZOOM` (0.5). A down seat leaves the frame (seat 1's focus alone when none is live). The trail decay resamples around a zoom change (`trailShiftFor`), and the swarm ship size follows the glyphs' square-root law of the zoom. The simulation never reads the camera. With two live seats the solo zoom controller idles (reset) and the camera call is exactly the co-op one; a zoom left outside the default range (solo zoom ending, a seat revived, the knob off, reduced motion on) eases back through `easeOutLimits` (`src/game/solo-zoom-step.js`) without a pop.
- **Colors and HUD**: `SEAT_COLORS` (`src/game/seat-colors.js`): seat 1 white, seat 2 green (body `[0.45, 1, 0.55]`, CSS `hsl(130, 90%, 65%)`); `src/render/swarm.js` draws one swarm per seat (`Game.scene().swarms`, each with its `bodyColor`; `scene.swarm` is the first). Projectiles stay untinted outside versus. `Game.state.seats` has one entry per seat (`ships`, `armed`, `tier`, `charge`, `railTurrets`, `railTier`, `down`, `reviveKills`, `reviveKillsNeeded`); every other `state` field is the local seat's (`localSeat.hudFields()`: seat 1's except on the online guest, where it is seat 2's). The HUD shows two seat rows in the seats' colors (`src/ui/hud-seat.js`, for example `P1 ▲ 12 BASE` and `P2 DOWN 40/100`) under the icons, for a whole two-player run only.

## Versus (stage 2)

Two seats fight each other in the neutral swarm, which stays in as hazard and food; co-op stays the default and plays exactly as before (every versus call is made only in versus).

- **Starts**: versus seats start `VERSUS_START_OFFSET` (10) either side of the world's center on x, 20 apart (`startsFor` in `src/game/versus-seats.js`, which `Game.placeSeats` and `resetModules` call; co-op keeps `SEAT_START_OFFSET`), so neither swarm is in the other's fire when a match begins.
- **Mode**: `Game.setMode('coop' | 'versus' | 'hives')` (`setGameMode` in `src/game/versus-seats.js`, `gameModeOf`) works in Ready only and versus and hives need two seats; `Game.mode`, `Game.versus` and `state.mode` read it. `setPlayerCount(1)`, `quitToTitle` and `setWatch` (through `removeSecondSeat`, `leaveVersus`) return to co-op. A mode change hands the simulation the projectile buffer (`Simulation.setVersusProjectiles(Projectiles.buffer)`, null to leave; carried across a capacity swap) and sets the pickup rotation (`PickupDirector.setRotation`: `VERSUS_PICKUP_ROTATION`, the rotation without the time warp, or `PICKUP_TYPE_NAMES`); an unchanged mode makes no call. `tests/game/versus-game.test.js` guards that only the mode switch calls `setVersusProjectiles`.
- **Title choice**: `createModeControl` (`src/ui/mode-choice.js`, pure `ModeChoice` and `modeItemView`) adds a mode item to the title menu (`createMenu`'s `modeItem`), always laid out beside Play (`.menu-mode`, absolutely placed so the list never moves) and second in the focus order; until player 2 joins it is ghosted (`MODE: SOLO`, `aria-disabled`, `.menu-disabled`, skipped by the focus ring, click and confirm do nothing), then reads `MODE: CO-OP`, `MODE: VERSUS` or `MODE: HIVES` and confirm or a click steps it through `MODE_CYCLE` (co-op, versus, hives, co-op). Player 2 leaving (and so a quit or the lab, which unseat player 2) returns it to co-op. `startRun` calls `game.setMode(modeControl.mode)` after `setPlayerCount`. Menu items take an `id`, a `className` and `enabled`; `menu.setItem(id, { label, enabled })` changes one in place.
- **Projectile owners**: in versus `fireSeats` passes each seat's index as the owner of its shots, gunner needles and beam (`Projectiles.queueSpawn(spawn, owner)`, `setBeam(beam, seat, owner)`; solo and co-op pass no owner argument at all), written as the seat plus 1 in projectile flag bits 2 and 3 (`projectileOwnerFlags`, `PROJECTILE_OWNER_SHIFT`; `projectileOwner` in `common.wgsl`). Rings made from an owned orb or sidewinder keep the flags.
- **PvP hits** (`versus_hits.wgsl`, `VersusHitPass` in `src/sim/versus-hits.js`, one thread per projectile slot, after player contact and only while versus projectiles are set with two seats): an owned projectile tests the opposing seat's alive ships whose hit bit is clear, when that seat is armed in the contact uniform (the contact pass's own `swarmBuffer`, so revive immunity and `debugInvulnerable` disarm it alike), and claims a ship by `atomicOr` on that seat's persistent swarm hit mask; only the claim that saw the bit clear kills it, so one projectile kills at most one ship, two projectiles never take one ship and a ship already hit never absorbs a shot. The CPU kills a claimed ship exactly as one an agent touched. Capsule kinds (bullet, scatter, rail needle, pulse orb, sidewinder) use the step's segment with reach radius plus ship radius plus 0.02 and stop on the hit (alive bit cleared; an orb or a sidewinder that hits a ship makes no ring); a pulse ring kills at most one ship per step inside the band it swept this step (the collide pass's band, widened by the ship radius) and goes on; a beam kills the hittable ship nearest its origin along its current ray (half width plus ship radius) on every 6th step only (`BEAM_HIT_STEP_INTERVAL`, at most 10 ships a second), ignoring the lab's reach scale. Storage buffers: projectiles and stats (2); uniforms: params and the contact swarm.
- **Ship contact** (`contactPairs` in `src/game/ship-contact.js`, pure; `applyShipContact` in `versus-seats.js`): after the flock step of a Playing update, every seat 1 ship in order takes the nearest unpaired seat 2 ship within `SHIP_CONTACT_DISTANCE` (twice `SWARM_SHIP_RADIUS`) and both die (`PlayerSwarm.killShip`), one for one, with the small loss shockwave; never while either seat or the run is invulnerable.
- **Kill credit**: `projectiles_collide.wgsl` adds each kill by an owned projectile to its owner's workgroup count, flushed into the cumulative stats words `ownerKills` (40 for seat 1, 41 for seat 2); `Simulation.readAllStats` adds `ownerKills` (`decodeOwnerKills`) and `ownerKillsSinceLastRead` (differences, wrapping, `lastOwnerKills` carried across a swap). In versus a scored frame grows only the seat whose projectiles made the kills (`growSwarms`); the score, multiplier and awards stay shared (one score of every kill).
- **Elimination**: no down and revive. `applyVersusHits` applies both seats' masks (agent contact and PvP alike) and `VersusMatch.judge` (`src/game/versus.js`, pure) ends the match when a seat has no ships: the other seat wins, both at once (one stats frame, or one contact step) is a draw. The end is the run's one death (`RunState.playerHit` at the wiped seat's last ship, seat 2's for a draw; every seat's weapons dropped, multiplier reset), then game over; `state.matchResult` is `{ winner, draw }` (null while the match runs, cleared by a restart) and the game over title reads `PLAYER n WINS` in that seat's CSS color, or `DRAW` (`gameOverBanner` in `src/ui/hud-seat.js`, `hud.showGameOver`'s third argument). The restart (a fresh press after `RESTART_GUARD_SECONDS`, Play again, the pause menu) plays versus again; quit to title returns to co-op.
- **Shared systems in versus**: no time warp pickup (rotation), no beam shards (the cadence is skipped, so beam time never starts), the escalation stays at `NEUTRAL_ESCALATION` (`applyEscalation` treats versus like watch mode, so the horde does not end the match), one shared bomb pool whose radial kill never touches ships (bombs kill neutral agents only), shared pickups anchored between the seats, the co-op camera and seek points.
- **Readability**: an owned projectile is drawn `OWNER_TINT_STRENGTH` (0.7) of the way toward its seat's body color at its own peak brightness (`tintForOwner` in `src/render/shaders/projectiles.wgsl`, `SEAT_ONE_TINT` and `SEAT_TWO_TINT` equal to `SEAT_COLORS` by `tests/render/projectile-owner-tint.test.js`); unowned projectiles are untouched.
- **Sparring dummy (diagnostic)**: `?debug=sparring` (off by default; `src/main.js` alone enables it) lets one person test versus and hives. `SeatJoin.seatVirtual()` (via `createCoopJoin({ sparring })`) seats seat 2 on `VIRTUAL_PAD_INDEX` (1000, an index no browser reports) from load: `playerCount` is 2 so the mode item offers CO-OP, VERSUS and HIVES, `readGamepadAt` reads neutral for it (it sits still at its start, never fires or bombs), and it survives `reset()`, a shoulder press, a disconnect and the lab round trip without the pause toast; seat 1 keeps the first real pad. The join banner reads `DEBUG SPARRING: PLAYER 2 IS A DUMMY ...`, the P2 status row `P2: SPARRING DUMMY (DEBUG)`, and a toast says so at load. `Game.setNeutralImmuneSeats([1])` marks seat 2 neutral-immune: `Simulation.contactUpload` clears its `armedSeats` bit (neutral agents cannot hit it) but keeps its `projectileArmedSeats` bit, so seat 1's projectiles and `applyShipContact` still kill its ships; revive immunity, `debugInvulnerable` and `debugInvulnerableSeats` still clear both masks. The dummy's ships stay present for hive capture (seat 2's start is not on a hive). `tests/game/sparring-guard.test.js` guards that only `main.js` enables it.

## Hives (stage 3)

A third two-seat mode (`GameMode.Hives`, `MODE: HIVES`): versus plus three capturable hives that turn the neutral swarm on the opponent. Everything versus does applies (`game.versus` is true, the far versus starts, projectile owners, PvP hits, ship contact, per-seat kill credit, elimination, the winner banner, no time warp, no beam shards, neutral escalation); plain versus, co-op and solo never set a hive gate or an agent target (`tests/game/hives-guard.test.js`).

- **State**: `VersusMatch` carries `hives` (`Hives` in `src/game/hives.js`, pure, injected time) in hives mode, null in plain versus; `VersusMatch.reset` (every run start) resets it to neutral; `hivesOf(game)` reads it. Tuning is one frozen object, `HIVE_TUNING` in `src/game/hive-tuning.js` (the lab is solo, so no lab knobs; tuning is a code edit).
- **Sites** (`hiveSites(bounds, VERSUS_START_OFFSET)`): seat 1's hive `sideOffset` (4.5) toward the top of the screen from seat 1's start (6, 4.5), seat 2's the same distance toward the bottom from its start (26, 13.5) and the contested one at the center (16, 9), point-symmetric about the center, radius `HIVE_RADIUS` 1.5. Entering hives calls `SpawnDirector.setFixedSites` with them (leaving calls it with null): the gates are the three sites, fully open from the first update, never drifting, closing or following the view; waves still pick among them by the safe radius but ignore the aim lane, and filling, continuous and surging work as for roaming gates; every row carries its `site`.
- **Capture** (`captureStep`, run by `updateHives` from `stepVersus` after ship contact, Playing only): a seat's live ships inside a hive's disc (`shipsInDisc` over `PlayerSwarm.slots`) minus the other seat's give the advantage; the seat with it builds progress at `advantage * captureRatePerShip` (1/60) per second, at most `maximumCaptureRate` (1/3), so 12 ships take 5 s and 20 or more 3 s. Progress has one capturer: the other seat first unwinds it; reaching 1 makes the capturer the owner (from neutral or taking it from the other seat); an owner with the advantage unwinds an attempt on its hive; equal ships freeze it; no ships decay it at `decayRate` (0.1) per second. A capture queues a 0.4 shockwave at the hive.
- **Aimed horde**: `hiveGateTable` gives each hive row `owner`, `target` (`hiveTarget`: the other seat) and `hive` ({ owner, capturer, progress }); `packSimParams` packs owner and target as seat plus 1 into `spawnGateOwnership` (`SimParams` byte 260, 4 bits per row; `packGateOwnership`). `spawn.wgsl` writes a gate spawn's row target into agent flag bits 16 and 17 (`AGENT_TARGET_SHIFT`, `AGENT_TARGET_MASK`; bit 0 is alive and bits 8 to 15 the spawn-in frames, every pass keeps the other bits and a spawn writes the whole word), and `steer.wgsl` seeks `seekPointFor` (`common.wgsl`): target 1 the first seek point, 2 the second while it is live, else exactly `nearestPlayerPosition`; the seek range fade stays on the nearest point. Neutral hives, wall band spawns and every other mode write 0. No shader gained a storage binding.
- **Feeding ground**: `projectiles_collide.wgsl` counts an owned kill within `HIVE_FEEDING_REACH_SCALE` (2) hive radii of a hive its owner holds (`onFeedingGround`, from the gate table in `SimParams`) into the cumulative stats words `ownerFeedingKills` (42, 43); `Simulation.readAllStats` adds `feedingKillsSinceLastRead` (`decodeFeedingKills`, `lastFeedingKills` carried across a swap) and `hiveGrowth` grows the seat by its owned kills plus its feeding kills once more (`feedingGrowth`, `feedingGrowthMultiplier` 2).
- **Hold win**: `HiveHold` counts how long one seat has owned every hive (a new holder starts from 0); at `holdSecondsToWin` (20) `VersusMatch.awardHold` sets `{ winner, draw: false, hives: true }` and `endMatch` (`src/game/match-end.js`, shared with elimination) runs the run's one death at the loser's swarm centroid, then game over with `PLAYER n WINS`. The restart plays hives again from neutral; one seat, quit and the lab return to co-op with roaming gates.
- **Readability**: the gate renderer draws a table with hive rows through its hive pipeline (`hiveFragment` in `gates.wgsl`, `HiveView` uniform at binding 1, `packHiveView`, `isHiveTable`; the plain gate pipeline and its 160-byte `GateView` are unchanged): neutral a pale dashed ring, owned a solid ring and haze in the owner's `SEAT_COLORS` body color, capture progress an arc in the capturer's color clockwise from the top of the screen. The HUD (`src/ui/hud-hives.js`, under the multiplier, `.hud-hives` in `hud.css`) lays out three pips (hive order: seat 1's, seat 2's, center) for a whole hives run only, filled in the owner's color or filling clockwise (a conic gradient) in the capturer's, and the countdown `Pn WINS IN s` in the holder's color, reserved by visibility. Every hive gets an edge indicator in its owner's color while off screen.

## Online (stage 4)

Two players on different networks play versus or hives over WebRTC by split authority: each machine runs the whole game and the things that must have one answer travel. Co-op, solo, the lab and the sparring dummy are unavailable online; with no session `Game.online` is null, no networking code runs and every existing call is made as before (only `src/ui/online-wiring.js` builds a session).

- **Roles and seats**: the player who makes the invite is the host, seat index 0 (white, left); the opener is the guest, seat index 1 (green, right), on both machines, so `PLAYER 2 WINS`, hive owners and projectile owners mean the same seat at both houses. `Game.localSeatIndex` (0 except on the guest), `localSeat`, `setLocalSeat(index)` (Ready only, `RangeError` outside the seats) and `framedFocuses(seats, fallbackIndex)` thread the local seat through `Game.player`/`swarm`/`weapons` and the other seat-1 getters, `inputFor` (the local seat reads `input.state`, never the one-pad `seatTwoState`), the HUD seat 1 fields, the fire log, the camera fallback focus and rumble (`rumblePadFor` in `padForSeat`, so rumble goes to the local seat's pad only). A seat the other machine drives has `seat.remote` true: `Game.update` reads no input for it, skips its steer, flock, fire, pickup collection and bomb, and `uploadSwarms` uploads it `invulnerable` (the existing `Simulation.setSwarmShips` option, which clears both `armedSeats` and `projectileArmedSeats` bits as revive immunity does), so local neutral agents chase its ships but never kill them or die on them, and local shots pass through them. The world is not mirrored; the camera is the couch one (`framingCenter`, `fitZoom`) over both seats' focuses.
- **The driver seam**: `OnlineGame` (`src/net/online-game.js`, `game.online`, null without a session; `attach(game)` needs two seats in Ready, marks the remote seat, sets `setFullRateLock(true)` and installs itself; `detach` undoes it) is the one adapter the game calls, always as `game.online?.` or `if (game.online)`. Hook points: `Game.update` (`applyRemoteSeats` after the local seats flock; `agePickups` in place of the guest's `pickups.update`; `observePickups`; `collectFor` in place of `collectFor(game, seat)`; `afterUpdate` as the last line; `requestRestart` at game over), `Game.spawnCounts` (the guest's `guestSpawns`), the scored stats frame (the guest does not `score.recordKills`), `Game.state` (`sharedHud` over the guest's score, multiplier and bombs; `offeredType` null on the guest), `fireSeats` in `seats.js` (`recordShots`, `recordBeam`, `replayRemoteFire`), `stepVersus`, `growSwarms` and `applyVersusHits` in `versus-seats.js` (online: `OnlineGame.stepVersus`, `growSwarms`, `applyHits`), and `pressBombs` in `src/game/bomb-step.js` (online: `pressBomb`). `src/game/pickup-collect.js` holds `applyCollection(game, seat, type, x, y, { arm })` (`arm` false on the machine that did not collect: the blast and shockwave only) and the couch `collectFor`.
- **Who decides what**: each machine decides its own ships' deaths, by its own neutral agents (the contact pass) and by the other player's shots replayed on its screen (`versus_hits.wgsl` against the local seat); `OnlineGame.applyHits` applies the local seat's mask alone and the other machine learns of a loss from the next snapshot (a 0.12 shockwave per remote ship lost). The host alone runs the `SpawnDirector` (the hive sites in hives, the roaming gates in plain versus), whose gate table and wave travel in every host snapshot; the guest runs no director (`guestSpawns` feeds its own `SpawnPressure` the host's wave, spawning nothing before the first host block), so how many agents spawn is each machine's own and where and when is the host's. The host alone runs capture, ownership, the hold and the hold win (`Hives`, `HiveHold`, `captureStep`, `updateHives`), and sends `hiveCaptured` for the guest's 0.4 shockwave. Ship contact: the host pairs its ships with its mirror of the guest's (`contactPairs`), kills its own, and names the dead slots of both seats in one `contactKills` event per update, each with the ship's generation so a kill aimed at a ship that died and regrew in that slot is ignored; the guest kills what is named and never pairs. Pickups (`src/net/pickup-ledger.js`, `online-pickups.js`): the host's `PickupDirector` is the only one (the versus rotation); `PickupLedger` gives each pickup an id (from 1, never reused in a session) announced as `pickupAnnounced` and `pickupExpired`; the host's seat takes what it reaches at once, the guest sends one `pickupClaim` per id, the host grants the first (`pickupCollected` with seat, type and the collector's point as f32), the collector arms its `WeaponState` and both play the blast at that point. The bomb pool is the host's `RunState`: a host press spends it at once, a guest press sends `bombRequest` with its swarm's centroid (its point with no ships), and the host spends and announces `bombAnnounced`; both clear the same disc, the f32 center and `ONLINE_BOMB_RADIUS` (`bombRadiusForView` of the unzoomed 16 by 9 view, `src/net/online-bombs.js`). The score, multiplier and awards are the host's (it scores the kills its own simulation counts, the guest's shots replayed there included); the guest shows them from the host state. Elimination: each machine reports its own wipe (`reportWipe`; the guest's `wiped` event carries its match time), the host's `MatchReferee` (`src/net/match-referee.js`) decides, two wipes within `DRAW_WINDOW_SECONDS` (0.2) being a draw and a lone wipe waiting that window plus the half round trip before it loses, and the host sends `result` (`{ winner, draw, hives }`); both then run `endMatch`. A hold win the host's `updateHives` ended is only announced.
- **What travels** (`src/net/protocol.js`: `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 1, `Channel`): one `RTCPeerConnection` with two pre-negotiated data channels, `state` (id 0, unordered, `maxRetransmits` 0) and `events` (id 1, reliable and ordered). Messages are binary (`ByteWriter` and `ByteReader` in `byte-buffer.js`, little-endian). A snapshot (`snapshot-codec.js`, message code 1, `buildSeatSnapshot` in `seat-snapshot.js`) goes out from each machine on every `SNAPSHOT_UPDATE_INTERVAL` (2nd) fixed update, so 30 a second, never on a timer: sequence, sender match time, point and velocity, aim, flags (firing, invulnerable, beam, host block, host state), the weapon readout, an alive bitmap over 256 slots with 16-bit positions over the world, 8-bit headings over [-PI, PI], a generation and a gunner station per ship, the beam when firing, and from the host only the gate table and wave in every snapshot and the `HostState` (`host-state.js`: each hive's owner, capturer and progress, the hold holder and seconds, the score, multiplier and bomb count, with its own sequence so a lost or reordered one never steps the guest back) in every `HOST_STATE_UPDATE_INTERVAL` (6th, 10 a second). `RemoteSeatDriver` (`remote-seat.js`) keeps the newest 32 snapshots and samples the remote seat `INTERPOLATION_DELAY_SECONDS` (0.1) behind the newest, extrapolating at most `MAXIMUM_EXTRAPOLATION_SECONDS` (0.1) then holding; a regrown slot (another generation) takes over with no blend, and `suppress` hides a ship this machine already knows is dead. Events (`event-codec.js`, codes from `FIRST_EVENT_CODE` 16 in the order of `EVENT_TYPES`): `hello`, `ping`, `pong`, `ready`, `titleMode`, `start`, `restartRequest`, `restart`, `pause`, `resumeRequest`, `resumeCountdown`, `quit`, `shots`, `contactKills`, `pickupAnnounced`, `pickupExpired`, `pickupClaim`, `pickupCollected`, `bombRequest`, `bombAnnounced`, `hiveCaptured`, `wiped`, `result` (a pickup event's type field is `pickupType`). `shots` is one event per update of the local seat's spawn records (shots, gunner needles) stamped with the sender's match time; `ShotRelay` (`shot-relay.js`) queues each with `Projectiles.queueSpawn(spawn, owner)` aged by its time in flight (moved along its velocity, its lifetime cut), so sidewinders and pulse orbs steer by the local density and differ a little per screen. The remote beam is set in its own slot from its snapshot. A snapshot, `shots` or `wiped` stamped more than `STALE_MATCH_MARGIN_SECONDS` (2) past the remote's match time now, and a message that does not decode, is dropped. Snapshots go out at 30 a second; the bandwidth is unmeasured.
- **Growth**: a seat grows by its own owned kills (hives: with its feeding kills once more, `hiveGrowth`) as its own machine counts them, scaled by `onlineGrowth` = kills times `DENSITY_REFERENCE_CAPACITY / capacity` before `addKills`, so a peer on a lower preset does not grow slower. Couch and solo growth are unchanged.
- **Clock and lifecycle**: `SessionClock` (`session-clock.js`) gives session time from the connection and match time advanced by game updates (frozen while paused); a `ping`/`pong` every `PING_INTERVAL_SECONDS` (1) from connection estimates the round trip and the offset from the best of the last 8 samples, and every snapshot and event carries its sender's match time. `SessionLifecycle` (`lifecycle.js`, pure, phases title, starting, playing, paused, countdown, lost, ended) and `OnlineSession` (`online-session.js`: roles, `markReady`, `setTitleMode`, `start(mode)`, `pause`, `resume`, `restart`, `quit`, `dispose`, `roundTripMilliseconds`, `waitingFor`, `endReason`, and an `onAction` stream that the page acts on; it builds its `OnlineGame`, owns the timers through an injected `setTimer`, and compares the `hello` version) run it. Each side sends `ready` once its loading and warm-up are done and the host's Play waits for both; the host's `start` carries the mode and a start moment `START_LEAD_SECONDS` (0.5) ahead on the session clock, both machines then `setPlayerCount(2)`, attach, `setMode`, `resetMatch()` and start when their clock reaches it, with no visible countdown. A restart (`restartRequest` from either, then the host's `restart`) from game over or the pause menu restarts both, also from Paused. A pause by either player (Escape, the button, Options, blur, a hidden tab, a pad dropping out, the rotate overlay) pauses both under the pause menu with the note `PLAYER n PAUSED`; either may resume, which runs the 3, 2, 1 countdown (`RESUME_COUNTDOWN_SECONDS`, `resumeCountdown` carries the end moment) on both and either may pause again during it; a pause is refused at Ready outside Starting and at game over. Quit by either ends the session for both (`quitToTitle` to the co-op title; the quitter's graceful close flushes its queued events first). When the transport reports disconnected both pause with `CONNECTION LOST` and wait `CONNECTION_LOST_SECONDS` (10) for recovery, then the match ends with a toast; a dropped match is not resumed. A version mismatch in `hello` ends both with "Both players need to reload the page." Liveness uses the connection's state and session timers, not the frame loop, because a hidden tab stops frames.
- **Quality**: `Game.setFullRateLock(true)` while attached holds the simulation at full rate (`setSimulationRate('half')` is remembered but `fullRateLocked` keeps the step interval 1), so a `phoneLow` device runs full rate for the session and the probe cannot step it into half. The two machines may differ in preset and capacity; capacity swaps keep working.
- **Screens** (`src/ui/online-wiring.js`, `online-panel.js`, `online-title.js`, `resume-countdown.js`, `online.css`): `createOnlineWiring` is the one place `main.js` reaches online play through and builds nothing until the ONLINE item or a `#join=` link asks. The ONLINE item (`onlineMenuItem`, id `online`, always present, absolutely placed left of Play mirroring the MODE item on the right so the couch title does not move) opens the Host panel: the page creates the connection, waits for ICE gathering (`ICE_GATHER_CAP_SECONDS` 3), `encodeSignal` compresses the offer (`CompressionStream('deflate-raw')`, base64url, in `signal-codec.js`) and `inviteUrl` puts it in the fragment of the page's own address as `#join=...` (the `debug` query parameter is dropped so a host's sparring flag never reaches the guest), with Copy invite link, a reply box, Paste reply (clipboard in one press), Enter or a paste. The guest opens the link (`openFromHash` clears the fragment at once and holds the join until the title is open) into the Join panel with Copy reply. The panel exists in both modes with every control laid out (a mode only changes text, visibility and ghosting), and an invite works once. `CONNECT_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (10) runs on the host from an accepted reply; on expiry it says "Your two networks cannot connect directly." and the host's panel returns with a fresh invite. The guest has no timeout: after Copy reply its panel reads "Waiting for your friend to paste your reply." until it connects or Cancel, because it cannot see the host's paste (its ICE checks start at once, and Chrome calls an unanswered answerer failed after about 15 s and connects once the host's checks arrive, so `createRtcConnection` keeps an answerer that has never opened connecting through failed and disconnected); the guest shows the network message only when its connection ends before it opens. Every panel press unlocks the audio inside the gesture (`onPress`); a failed connect leaves the panel up holding the title (no couch start under it, Play ghosted in place) until Cancel or ONLINE dismisses it. Connected, the title shows one status line in seat 2's color (host: `PLAYER 2 CONNECTED ONLINE (n MS)`; guest: `CONNECTED TO PLAYER 1 ONLINE (n MS)`), the ONLINE item reads DISCONNECT on both, the host's mode item cycles `ONLINE_MODES` (hives, versus) from HIVES, the host's Play reads `WAITING FOR PLAYER 2 TO LOAD` (or `WAITING FOR YOUR GAME TO LOAD` while its own page is the one loading, from `waitingFor`) until both are ready, and the guest's Play reads `WAITING FOR THE HOST TO START` with the host's mode shown read-only. While a connection exists couch joining, the mode item's couch rule, the Lab (`LabMode`'s `entryBlocked`) and the pad status rows are out of play, and the first connection calls `setNeutralImmuneSeats([])` with the toast "DEBUG SPARRING is off online" (the dummy stays off until a reload). The pause menu has an always laid out `note` line (`setNote`, `menu-note-hidden` by visibility), and its Resume (id `resume`) is ghosted in place while the note reads `CONNECTION LOST`. A start or restart action that lands while the page is hidden or has no focus (`document.hidden`, `document.hasFocus()`) calls `session.pause()` at once, so a guest away in a chat window pauses both instead of leaving the host to play alone. The peer quitting toasts "Player n left the match." in a match and "Player n left." at the connected title. `window.__murmuration.online` exposes `{ session, inviteUrl, replyText, phase }`.
- **Networking**: no server, no signaling relay, no TURN, no short codes: public STUN only, set in one place (`ICE_SERVERS` in `src/net/ice-configuration.js`, `stun:stun.l.google.com:19302` then `stun:stun.cloudflare.com:3478`). Anti-cheat is a non-goal (friends, total trust). Phones with touch are expected to work but are not tested online.

## Audio and probe rules

- Audio: notes are scheduled at least 100 ms ahead against `AudioContext.currentTime` through `StepSequencer` (lookahead 0.1 s, a timer only advances the schedule); never `setTimeout`-driven notes. The gesture that starts the run is the single unlock (`audio.unlock()` inside the event handler; a gamepad press is not an activation). Music tempo is 124 to 134 bpm, changed on bar starts only. Tests fake the audio context and inject time.
- Probe contract: the frame-time probe (`FrameTimeProbe` through `QualityController`) runs during Playing only and never during beam time or the `BEAM_TIME_PROBE_GRACE_SECONDS` (4, game time, `Game.probeGraceSeconds`, cleared by `cancelBeamTime`) after it (`Game.acceptsProbeFrames`, so the surge and its surplus crowd cannot step a preset down), target 10 ms, at most one step per 3 s; it steps down particles, then bloom, then capacity (a preset down). `?probe=off` or `?capacity=` disables it; every browser check passes `probe=off`. Presets change counts and budgets only, never gameplay.

## Pass order

Each animation frame `FrameLoop` runs zero or more fixed update steps, then one render. Each is its own queue submission.

- Update (`Simulation.step`): spawn (in the discs of the step's emitting spawn gates, overflowing outward where the previous step's coarse field is crowded, a gate spawn taking its row's seek target into agent flag bits 16 and 17; in the wall band, with the density bias, when no gate emits), grid count, grid scan, grid scatter, coarse field downsample, coarse field blur, density readback (every 15th step, `src/sim/density-readback.js`), steer (seek toward `seekPointFor`: the nearest seek point unless the agent's target bits name one), integrate, kill-all (count, then kill; world-wide only in the step after `requestKillAll`, the run start and the death clear, or radial after `requestRadialKill`, the bomb around the swarm and the pickup blast around the ship: a `KillArea` uniform at binding 6 limits it to agents within a radius; of two radial requests in one step the larger radius wins, and a kill-all in the same step wins over both; kills are unscored), registered passes (`Simulation.registerPass`; `Projectiles.encode` runs projectile step, collide, rings and beam here), player contact with the hit latch (once `setPlayerState` has been called; the contact uniform holds up to 512 slots, one 256-slot half per seat, bit k of `armedSeats` arms seat k, and `seatCount` seats' workgroups are dispatched), versus hits (`versus_hits.wgsl`, only while `setVersusProjectiles` holds a buffer and two seats are present: owned projectiles claim the opposing seat's hit bits; see Versus), debug kill (only while armed; only the browser checks arm it), particle step (recounts the pool's live particles), stats, stats copy into the staging ring. Every kill asks for `killBurstParticles` (12) sparks plus `killFragmentParticles` (3) heavy fragments from one per-step budget, the pool slots free after the last particle step; kill-all thins its bursts evenly when a clear would outrun it and dims them to a light budget, and in the death clear's step it first emits the death flood (a white flash and `deathFloodParticles` at the ship). The step clears the pool's per-step claims and only the stats buffer's per-frame prefix; its persistent tail (`playerHitSequence`, `hitLatch`, the cumulative scored `kills` counter, both swarm hit masks and the per-seat owned kill counters) survives (the stats buffer is `STATS_WORD_COUNT` = 48 words: the first mask at words 24 to 31, the second at 32 to 39, `ownerKills` at 40 and 41, `ownerFeedingKills` at 42 and 43, words 44 to 47 spare), and a capacity swap (`commitCapacity`, its own submission between two frames) copies it into the new simulation along with the compacted alive agents, the second seek point and the seats' contact tables, then builds the new simulation's grid and coarse field so the next render can draw it. The projectile step counts pulse ring detonations into the prefix (`ringsThisFrame`, `lastRingX`, `lastRingY`).
- Render (`Renderer.render`, around the camera that `main.js` sets with `Viewport.setCamera(game.camera.sample(alpha))` before each render): warp grid layer (the whole world's lattice, then the world border: `WorldBorder`, `src/render/world-border.js` and `worldborder.wgsl`, one quad over the world rectangle whose fragment shader lights the outline as a whole: a square-cornered Chebyshev core of `BORDER_CORE_HALF_PIXELS` plus a glow of `BORDER_GLOW_PIXELS` (Euclidean, so it rounds outside corners), both device pixels scaled by the device pixel ratio, in the same cleared layer so the bloom lights it and it cannot smear; nothing is drawn outside the world; `WORLD_WARP_LATTICE` at the one-screen spacing, or `PHONE_WARP_LATTICE` with the one-screen line count on a phone; with this frame's shockwave impulses), trails decay (reading the previous scene shifted by the camera's movement since the last frame in whole device pixels, `trailOriginFor`), spawn gates (`Simulation.spawnGates`), glyphs (extrapolated from the newest step, or at half simulation rate interpolated from `previousAgentsBuffer`), projectiles, pickups, particles, ship and charge ring, bloom, composite. `Game.scene()` supplies the projectiles, pickups and ship; the ship hides from death until respawn, and at game over. After it `main.js` updates the HUD and the edge indicators (`src/ui/edgeIndicators.js`: DOM chevrons on the view edge for off-screen pickups and the telegraphed wave gate).

## Frame order of operations

One `Game.update(deltaSeconds)` is one fixed step (full comments at the top of `src/game/game.js`):

Steps 1 to 4 and 6 run per seat in seat order (`Game.seats`: one in solo; seat 1 reads `input.state`, seat 2 `input.seatTwoState`; online the local seat, whichever index, reads `input.state` and the remote seat nothing, `inputFor`).

1. Input: `input.update`; in Ready, the first gesture (fire, bomb, movement or stick) of either seat starts the run, and a start clears the world with one kill-all. A rising bomb edge of a seat that is not down, accepted by `RunState.useBomb` (one shared pool), requests a radial kill before this step, centered on that seat's swarm centroid with a radius of 1.2 view half-diagonals (`bombRadiusForView` in `src/game/camera.js`).
2. `PlayerShip.update` (Playing only; a remote seat online steers and flocks nowhere, and `applyRemoteSeats` writes it from the other machine's snapshots after the local seats flock); the mouse aims from the ship to the pointer unless a stick or touch aim is pushed; with the beam armed the aim turns at most `beamTurnRevolutionsPerSecond` (default 1.5, lab knob, 0 is instant) via `turnAimToward`. A down seat does not move. After the swarms move, `Game.followCamera` updates the camera toward `framingCenter` of the live seats' `cameraFocus` values (each half the seat's controlled point, half its swarm centroid) and its zoom toward `fitZoom` (1 with one live seat) or, with one live seat and the lab's solo zoom enabled and not reduced motion, toward `soloZoomOptions` (`src/game/solo-zoom-step.js`: `sampleZoomPressure` samples `Game.densityAt` at the swarm and a ring just outside the view, the controller holds its target for `SOLO_ZOOM_BOMB_HOLD_SECONDS` (1 s of game time) after a bomb, during an easing time warp and outside Playing, and Game over eases out); a start, a quit and a respawn cut it there, and watch mode holds it still. In versus, after the swarms move, touching opposing ships die one for one (`applyShipContact`, see Versus).
3. (Online the guest runs no director: `agePickups` shows the host's pickups, and a seat's reach goes through `collectFor` as a claim, see Online.) `PickupDirector.update` (anchored on `pickupAnchor`, with `densityAt`: a spawn takes the lowest-density of up to 8 candidates on the placement annulus, 3 to 5 units for the 16:9 view and scaled by the view half-diagonal, inside the world and a view-sized rectangle around the player), then collection at each seat that is not down (its point, else its ships) into that seat's `WeaponState.collect`, before charge expiry; a rail pickup adds a turret, a gunner ship promoted on the next swarm update (`railTurrets`, up to `MAXIMUM_RAIL_TURRETS` = 12, stations 30 degrees apart around the rear of the aim; later pickups raise `railTier`) and leaves the armed weapon, its tier and charge alone (owner ruling R29). A collection also plays the pickup effect and, with `Game.pickupBlastRadius` above 0 (default 1.5, `setPickupBlastRadius`), calls `Simulation.requestRadialKill` at the ship and queues a 0.5 warp impulse; radius 0 skips the blast and queues the 0.15 impulse. A time warp pickup bypasses `WeaponState.collect`: it starts or extends the warp (`TimeWarp.start`), plays `pickup('timewarp')` and queues a 0.6 impulse in place of the 0.5 or 0.15 one (the blast still fires). The recovery guard's pity spawns always offer a weapon: on the time warp slot they take the next slot's weapon, and the guard is paused during beam time (the armed weapon is the beam, never base). A beam shard (type `beamshard`, `BEAM_SHARD_TYPE`) is a `persistent` pickup: it never ages or fades, no bomb or pickup blast touches it, and it is drawn in the beam hue (`PICKUP_SHAPE_SHARD`). It bypasses `WeaponState.collect` like the time warp: `collectShardFor` (`src/game/beam-time-seats.js`) counts it in `BeamShards`, plays `pickup('beamshard')` and returns true when it was the last one needed, which starts beam time, plays `pickup('beamtime')` and queues a `BEAM_TIME_IMPULSE_STRENGTH` (1) shockwave in place of the 0.5 one (the blast still fires); the BEAM TIME! title (`beam-time-title.js`) shows on the rising edge of `state.beamTime`. A shard collected while beam time runs is consumed with no effect.
4. (Online each shot the local seat queues also goes out in a `shots` event and the remote seat's arrive aged, `replayRemoteFire`.) For each seat that is not down, `WeaponState.update` (armed kind, tier, charge and cue cadence; its own volley spawns are not queued), then `SwarmFire.distribute`: the ships' per-ship shots, then the gunners' rail spawns (`turretSpawns` from `GunnerFire` through `distribute`, at the rail interval while firing, beside any weapon including the beam), go to `Projectiles.queueSpawn` and the beam to `setBeam` (with the seat as owner in versus only; its own slot per seat with two seats; cleared on the expiry frame, which keeps the turrets). One `weaponFire` cue per firing frame: the armed weapon's, or `'rail'` at the turret tier when only the turrets fired. During beam time (`tickBeamTime` counts it in real time, pausing keeps it) every seat not down has the beam armed at `BEAM_TIME_TIER` (1) by `WeaponState.beginBeamTime`, which stashes the kind, tier and charge; the charge does not drain, a weapon pickup collected meanwhile changes the stash (`#collectInto`), the ships fire no projectiles (the single centroid beam per seat stays as it is) and the rail turrets keep firing; `endBeamTime` restores the stash and plays the expiry cue.
5. (Online the guest takes the host's gate table and wave: `guestSpawns`, no `SpawnDirector` update.) `Simulation.setPlayerState` with the first live seat's point (`setPlayer` only in Ready, so contact stays off until start; with two seats also `setSecondPlayer` or `clearSecondPlayer`), then the spawn director (`SpawnDirector.update` with the camera's view: gates open on a ring 1 to 1.4 view half-diagonals from the view center, just outside the view and inside the world, drift, and close, also when left more than 2 half-diagonals behind; one wave at a time from a gate outside the safe radius and the aim lane; no waves in Ready, where every gate trickles) and spawn pressure (`SpawnPressure.update` with the latest stats, the step's sequence, so spawns still in flight are not requested twice, and the director's wave, so refills bank between waves and pour out during them; both paused while dying and respawning, when the step gets no gates) and its ramped seek scale and spawn distance (`setSeekScale`, `setSpawnDistanceFraction`; the ramp restarts on start and respawn and is retuned through `SpawnPressure.setRamp`). Before the director's update `applyEscalation` (`src/game/escalation-step.js`) applies the difficulty escalation (`Game.escalation`, one per run, shared by the seats; `src/game/escalation.js`), whose clock is `SpawnPressure.secondsPastRamp` (the ramp's warped elapsed time past `rampSeconds`, so it restarts with the ramp, a time warp slows it and a capacity swap keeps it); with `t` = `rate` times that: refill multiplier `exp(t / 300)` (capped at 100; `SpawnPressure.setEscalation` `refillMultiplier`), the plain alive target from the ramp's ceiling 0.1 toward `ceilingFraction` 0.2 of capacity (`1 - exp(-t / 400)`; `targetMultiplier`, the beam time surge still on top with its 40 percent cap), seek multiplier `1 + (2 - 1)(1 - exp(-t / 360))` on the ramp's seek scale (`setSeekScale(pressure.seekScale * seekMultiplier)`; above about 1.67 a Chaser seeks at full speed and the steer clamps mute the rest), a shift of up to 0.35 of the Chaser share to the Predator (`1 - exp(-t / 480)`, `escalatedShares`, passed to the director and the pressure; `Simulation.shares` stays as set) and the wave period times `1 / (1 + t / 600)` with the burst fraction times `exp(t / 300)` times that (`SpawnDirector.setEscalation`; the period never below the shorter of `waveSeconds` and the schedule's own, the burst at most 1). At rate 1: target 0.126, 0.159, 0.170 and 0.186 of capacity, refill 0.22, 0.50, 0.74 and 2.0 of the target per second, seek 1.28, 1.63, 1.74 and 1.89, Predator share 0.12, 0.22, 0.25 and 0.30 and wave period 1.33, 1.0, 0.89 and 0.7 s at minutes 4, 8, 10 and 15. Every lever is exactly neutral (`NEUTRAL_ESCALATION`) during the ramp, at `rate` 0 (the lab's off), outside a Playing run, in watch mode and in versus; there is no enemy speed lever. Then, in a Playing run outside watch mode and versus, the shard cadence (`spawnShardsForWaves`: `BeamShards.onWaves` reads `SpawnDirector.waveSequence`, the count of waves started since the director's reset, and every `BEAM_SHARD_WAVE_INTERVAL` (10) waves asks for one shard while the count plus the waiting shards stays under `BEAM_SHARDS_NEEDED` (6); `placeShard` puts it off screen, inside the world and clear of gates and of the shards already waiting, preferring the anchor's reach, with, when the view covers the world, the inset corner or edge midpoint whose nearest neighbour (the anchor or a waiting shard) is farthest as the fallback, so waiting shards spread instead of stacking; a lower sequence, a director reset (`rebase`) or beam time yields none), then each seat's ships are uploaded for contact (`uploadSwarms`, with its revive immunity) and the simulation step is submitted with the director's gate table (`SpawnDirector.spawnGates`, through `hiveGateTable`, which in hives adds each hive's owner, target and capture state to its row). During a time warp (`TimeWarp`, started by a `timewarp` pickup) the director, spawn pressure and the simulation get the step's delta times `timeWarp.scale` while the projectile step gets the unscaled delta (`SimParams.projectileDt`); the warp timer counts real time, pausing keeps it, and death, a new run and quit to title end it. During beam time (`BeamTime`, 12 s of real time, `BEAM_TIME_SECONDS`) the spawn surge applies (`SpawnPressure.setSurge`, set every step from `Game.beamTimeSurge`, default `DEFAULT_BEAM_TIME_SURGE` 3, range 1 to 6): it multiplies the alive target (capped at `BEAM_TIME_SURGE_CEILING_FRACTION`, 40 percent of capacity, unless the plain target is higher) and also the refill rate, so refills run at about the surge squared (9 times at the default) until the target is reached; the director is told `surging` so every open gate emits, and `SpawnPressure.update` gets a null wave so the wave bank is bypassed. The shard count stays full while beam time runs and resets when it ends (`BeamShards.finish`) and at every death, new run and quit to title (`cancelBeamTime`, no cue).
6. (Online each machine grows and hits its own seat alone, scaled by `onlineGrowth`, and the guest does not score.) Every ready stats frame (`readAllStats`, oldest first): kills (`killsSinceLastRead`, the difference of the cumulative counter) score once per stats frame and only while Playing, kills grow every seat's swarm that is not down and count toward each down seat's revive (`growSwarms`, `reviveSeats`, which brings a seat back beside the first live seat; in versus each seat grows by its own `ownerKillsSinceLastRead` and nothing revives; in hives its `feedingKillsSinceLastRead` count once more, `hiveGrowth`), awards add bombs (lives are dropped), ring detonations queue a shockwave, and each seat's hit mask (`applySwarmHits`) kills its hit ships with a small shockwave each: a seat wiped while another has ships goes down, and when no seat is left `RunState.playerHit` is offered the death (which drops every seat's weapons and turrets, ends a time warp and resets the multiplier); in versus `applyVersusHits` kills the hit ships and a seat at 0 ships ends the match through the same death (see Versus).
7. (Online `OnlineGame.afterUpdate` ends the update: the match clock, a snapshot every 2nd update, the queued events.) `RunState.update` drains actions: clear the world (the death clear with the death flood, a world-wide kill-all) and projectiles, queue bomb and death shockwaves, respawn.

Watch mode (the lab; `Game.setWatch(fraction)`, `null` leaves it) keeps the phase Ready and disables the gesture start, so the Playing-only work of steps 2 to 4 and 6 (player movement, pickup collection, fire, scoring) never runs and contact stays off (`setPlayer` only); step 2 instead moves the player point to follow the pointer (or the move stick) so the swarm chases it, and step 5 runs a watch `SpawnPressure` with full seek that holds the alive target at `fraction` of capacity, with the director in continuous mode (no waves). `Game.start()` (the lab's Play) leaves watch mode too, and leaving the lab restores the state captured on entry (`captureLabState`).

The loading screen (`src/ui/loading.js`) covers boot and the warm-up: the first rendered frames, until at least 30 have run with the latest 10 each under 34 ms, or 3 s have passed (`WarmUp`, fed `deltaSeconds`). Until it finishes the run cannot start (`startsOnGesture` stays false, polled menu actions are dropped) and `window.__murmuration.ready` stays false.

While paused (`Game.pause`, from Escape, the HUD pause button, gamepad Options, blur, a hidden tab or the rotate overlay; only from Playing, Dying or Respawning), `Game.update` returns at once: no input read, no step, no stats read, no timers, so scores, lives and every sequence guard are exactly as they were and a hit in flight is handled after `resume()` by the usual rules; `state.phase` reads `paused`, a bomb or fire held through the pause needs a fresh press, and the main loop skips `audio.update` and ducks the music (`setPaused`). The title menu sets `Game.startsOnGesture = false` while it shows, so only its own start (button, arena tap, Enter, Space, face button) starts the run; `Game.quitToTitle` returns to Ready.

A capacity change (`Game.setCapacity` through `CapacitySwap`) is prepared (`Simulation.prepareCapacity`: buffers and pipelines) while the old simulation keeps stepping; commit and install then run synchronously between two updates, so play never pauses and no step is lost. A prepare that has not settled after `timeoutMilliseconds` (10000, on a monotonic clock) is ended by `CapacitySwap.expire`, which `Game.render` polls every frame: the request rejects with an Error named `CapacitySwapTimeout`, the pending flag clears so the probe and later swaps work again, and a prepare that resolves late is discarded. `settleApply` in `src/game/quality.js` applies a preset only when the swap succeeded. At half simulation rate (`Game.setSimulationRate('half')`, the `phoneLow` preset) the spawn pressure and simulation step of step 5 run every other update with both updates' delta; everything else still runs every update. `QualityController` (`src/game/quality.js`) takes frame times from `src/gpu/frame-timer.js` while Playing and applies presets between frames. After each update `main.js` hands `Game.freshStats` (the newest frame read this update, kills summed), the run phase and the multiplier to the audio hook (`IntensityTracker`: survival time, multiplier and kill rate); the game plays its cues itself through the hook's `cues` (`Game.create({ effects })`: bomb, death, pickup, expiry and `weaponFire(kind, tier)`, each an effect plus the matching music cue). Audio unlocks inside the gesture that starts the run (canvas pointerdown or touchstart, a key, or `audio.unlock()` from a button).

Event order inside the GPU step follows the pass order above: bomb kill-all or pickup blast, projectile kills, player contact. A pickup collected in the frame of charge expiry wins. A sidewinder detonates on its first kill into a half-size pulse ring and a pulse orb on its first kill into a full one (`projectiles_collide.wgsl`; rings pierce; an orb that meets nothing bursts at the end of its flight, at the arena edge or in a coarse cell denser than its `extra`); every other projectile kind pierces. GPU events carry `frameSequence`; the CPU ignores hits that predate the current respawn.
