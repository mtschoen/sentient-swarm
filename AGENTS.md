# Agent instructions

## Global constraints (binding)

- Plain JavaScript ES modules and WGSL. No TypeScript. No runtime dependencies. Vite and Playwright are dev dependencies only.
- Shaders are loaded with `fetch(new URL('./shaders/<name>.wgsl', import.meta.url))` so the page also runs from any static server without Vite.
- Full-word identifiers (`maximum`, not `max`; `position`, not `pos`) in JavaScript. WGSL may use `vec2f` style built-in names but user identifiers follow the same rule.
- No em-dashes (U+2014) anywhere, including comments and docs.
- Every source file under about 500 lines, or carrying a one-line note at the top saying why.
- No hard-coded machine paths. No wall-clock assertions in tests; inject time.
- Mobile WebGPU limits are designed for: at most 8 storage buffers bound per shader stage; agent data interleaved in 16-byte aligned structs with no `vec3` in storage layouts; `@workgroup_size` at most 256; workgroup memory at most 16 KB; timestamp queries optional.
- Neighbor contributions are averaged, never summed. Per-agent fine-pass neighbor sampling is capped at `MAXIMUM_NEIGHBOR_SAMPLES = 32`.
- The world is 32 by 18 world units, two views wide and high (`WORLD_WIDTH`, `WORLD_HEIGHT` in `src/sim/world.js`, re-exported by `layout.js` and substituted into `common.wgsl` through `SHADER_CONSTANTS`; the coarse field is the world in 0.25-unit cells, 128 by 72, and `gridDimensions` keeps the neighbor grid within `MAXIMUM_GRID_CELLS` = 65536 by raising the cell above the neighbor radius only where needed, which at this size is `desktopUltra` alone: 0.08 becomes about 0.0938). The view is the largest rectangle of the window's aspect inside a 16 by 9 screen (`viewSizeForAspect`), centered on the follow camera (`src/game/camera.js`, `Viewport.setCamera`, `Viewport.viewBounds`) and mapped to the whole canvas so the game fills the window at any aspect between 1:4 and 4:1 (letterboxed beyond that). The camera follows the player swarm (critically damped, 0.30 s smooth time, a dead zone of 10 percent of the view, clamped so the view may extend past the world's edge by `CAMERA_OVERSHOOT_FRACTION` (0.12) of its extent on that axis, so the border is seen coming and a swarm pressed against an edge sits clear of the screen edge; an axis where the view is as large as the world stays centered; cut on start, quit and respawn, still in watch mode) at zoom 1 in solo; with two seats it centers on the live seats' focuses and eases its zoom (`Camera.update` `targetZoom`, `CAMERA_ZOOM_SMOOTH_SECONDS` 0.5) between 1 and `MINIMUM_CAMERA_ZOOM` (0.5, the whole world) to fit them (`fitZoom`, `framingCenter`; see Co-op), so the view is `viewSize / zoom` (`Game.view`; `Viewport.setCamera({ centerX, centerY, zoom })`) and `Viewport.visibleBounds` is the world rectangle on screen, the one the edge indicators use; the simulation never reads it. The playable world bounds (walls, clamps, spawns, pickups, projectile edges and the beam's reach: `Game.worldBounds`, `Simulation.setWorldBounds`) are the whole world at every aspect, and the alive target is a fraction of capacity alone, so every aspect plays the same world with the same count. The view (`Game.setView`) sets the spawn gate ring, the minimum spawn distance (a fraction of the view width, `Simulation.setSpawnViewWidth`), the pickup reach and the bomb radius. Enemies seek the nearest live player (one or two seek points) from anywhere in the world. Renderer works in device pixels.
- Species count is 5. Coarse field is 128 by 72 cells (the world in 0.25-unit cells) with 8 float channels (5 species densities, 1 threat, 2 padding). Shipped shares are 0.9 Chaser, 0.03 Orbiter, 0.02 Skittish, 0 Lattice, 0.05 Predator (`DEFAULT_SHARES`, owner ruling R38); the lab's `equalFive` mix restores equal shares.
- Commit after every task with a message in the imperative.

## Player swarm

The player is a swarm of ships, not a single ship; `Game.seats` holds one `PlayerSeat` (`src/game/player-seat.js`) per player, and this section describes one seat (the Co-op section covers two); where the sections below still describe the single ship, its hit latch, lives or respawn, this section wins.

- The player-controlled point (`PlayerShip`) moves as the ship did and cannot be hit. `PlayerSwarm` (`src/game/player-swarm.js`, CPU) flies 12 small white ships (`INITIAL_SWARM_SHIPS`, `?swarm=N` overrides; drawn and hit at `SWARM_SHIP_SIZE`) up to 256 (`MAXIMUM_SWARM_SHIPS` in `src/sim/layout.js`) around it, one more per 100 scored kills (`KILLS_PER_SWARM_SHIP`); award lives are dropped, bombs stay. Each ship is its own agent flocking by the enemy Chaser's steering rules ported to the CPU (`src/game/swarm-flocking.js`: seek toward its slot group's roaming waypoint near the point, separation, cohesion and pressure from its neighbours, an acceleration cap), plus a per-slot temperament from `slotHash`, wander, a leash to the point and attack runs along the aim while firing (the attack bias swings along and against the aim on a `SWARM_ATTACK_SECONDS` cycle whose phase is evenly spaced over the slots, `attackPhaseFor`, so a steady share of the swarm faces the aim at any moment instead of the ships running in lockstep). A ship flies along its course (its velocity's direction) and points along its heading: while firing, `faceStep` (`src/game/swarm-facing.js`) turns the heading off the course toward the aim by up to `SWARM_FACING_LIMIT_RADIANS` (180 degrees less the fire cone, 145), squeezing the courses into the fire cone in proportion (`facingFor`) and turning at most `SWARM_FACING_TURN_RADIANS_PER_SECOND` (`SWARM_FACING_RETURN_RADIANS_PER_SECOND`, five revolutions a second, while the heading is outside the fire cone, so a swing of the aim does not silence the whole swarm), so ships flying across or straight away from the aim still shoot at it (a lower limit shuts out the courses nearest to straight away); not firing, the heading returns to the course. `SwarmFire` (`src/game/swarm-fire.js`) fires each shot as ONE ship firing ONE projectile (scatter: 1, 2, 3 pellets by tier) from its own position along its own heading, only within 35 degrees of the aim and after the ship's own cooldown. What a shot is comes from `swarmShotRecipe(kind, tier)` (`src/game/swarm-shots.js`: radius, lifetime, jitter, fan, homing); how often comes from the rate law in `src/game/swarm-fire-rate.js`: `aggregateShotsPerSecond = baseShotsPerSecond * (shipCount / 12) ^ exponent`, each ship's interval being the ship count over that aggregate times a rate-neutral 0.8 to 1.2 per-slot jitter, with cooldowns carrying their remainder (base 65 per second at 12 ships and about 220 at 256, exponent 0.4 for base and scatter; pulse and sidewinders a fixed aggregate by tier across ships in rotation). `WeaponState` still owns the armed kind, tier, charge, expiry and the `weaponFire` cue cadence, but its volley spawns are not queued by the game; turrets fire from one ship per volley, full size, and the beam from the centroid. Randomness is the `random` injected into `SwarmFire`.
- Contact: `Simulation.setSwarmShips(ships, radius, { seat, invulnerable })` stores one slot table per seat (`Simulation.swarmSeats`) and the step uploads them to one contact uniform of `MAXIMUM_CONTACT_SLOTS` (512) slots, seat k's slot s being slot `k * MAXIMUM_SWARM_SHIPS + s`, with an `armedSeats` bit per seat that is not invulnerable; `player_collide.wgsl` runs one workgroup per slot of `seatCount` seats, walks the grid around its ship and sets the slot's bit in the persistent 8-word `swarmHitMask` stats words (seat 2's slots in `secondSwarmHitMask`, words 32 to 39); `PlayerSwarm.applyHits` guards stale and reused slots by each ship's first uploaded step. The GPU hit latch and `playerHitSequence` are unused. Losing the last ship of the last seat still in the run is the run's one death (`RunState` with one life): death clear, then game over. The HUD lives readout shows the swarm size; `src/render/swarm.js` draws the swarm, each ship and the point extrapolated by the same alpha as `Camera.sample` (`PlayerSwarm.shipsAt`, `blendedPoint` in `src/game/swarm-blend.js`), so they cannot judder against the follow camera and stamp that into the trails. `tests/browser/swarm-spike.mjs` is the browser evidence run (not part of `check:gpu`); `tests/browser/player.mjs` (in `check:gpu`) checks the swarm contact pass and hit mask, kill-all and the pickup blast.
- **Fire log (diagnostic)**: `?debug=fire` shows on screen why the swarm went silent while fire was held, for a device with no console. `FireLog` (`src/game/fire-log.js`, pure, sampled once per `Game.update` through `Game.setFireLog`) keeps the newest releases of the fire input, each with the touch event that came last (`lastEvent` of `src/input/touch.js`: start, end, cancel, blur, ignored), the newest thin stretches (quarter-second windows of held fire with under half the ship shots the armed weapon's law gives, for fire that sputters without stopping; the status line counts them against all judged windows) and the newest silences (fire held while Playing with no ship shot for longer than the armed weapon explains), each with the reason from `SwarmFire.report` (ships, in the cone, off cooldown, fired, the law's rate), the aim and the movement; `src/ui/fire-debug.js` draws its lines under the HUD (`.fire-debug` in `hud.css`, never takes a touch) and `window.__murmuration.fireLog` exposes it.
- **Bee motion** (`src/game/swarm-motion.js`, fixed constants): the ships hover about the point like bees. `SWARM_TIGHTNESS` (2) adds a harmonic well toward the point (desired speed `TIGHTNESS_WELL_RATE * SWARM_TIGHTNESS * distance`, capped at the ship's maximum speed) and shrinks the roaming reach by `SWARM_ROAM_SCALE` = `1 / (1 + SWARM_TIGHTNESS)`; `SWARM_BUZZ` (1.5) adds a per-slot random velocity of `SWARM_BUZZ * BUZZ_SPEED_SHARE * cruise speed` (`buzzDirection`, redrawn 3 to 7 times a second) to the ship's movement directly (as a steering input the steering response and the acceleration cap smoothed it away); `SWARM_FOLLOW` (1) is the share of the point's velocity (`PlayerSwarm`, smoothed over `FOLLOW_SMOOTH_SECONDS`, capped at `SWARM_MAXIMUM_SPEED`) that carries the flock: `flockStep` moves every ship by that carry before steering, a ship's `velocityX` and `velocityY` are its velocity in the carried frame and its course is the direction of the whole movement, so the flock behaves around a moving point as around a still one.

## Co-op (stage 1)

Two players share one screen and one score against the neutral swarm; solo is one seat and plays exactly as before (every co-op call with one seat is the solo call).

- **Seats**: `Game.seats` holds one or two `PlayerSeat`s (`MAXIMUM_PLAYER_SEATS` = 2, `src/sim/layout.js`); `Game.setPlayerCount(1 | 2)` works in Ready only, and `quitToTitle` and `setWatch` return the game to one seat (`removeSecondSeat`). A seat owns its `PlayerShip`, `PlayerSwarm`, `SwarmFire`, `WeaponState`, held fire and bomb levels, `down` flag and revive immunity; `Game.player`, `swarm`, `swarmFire`, `weapons`, `swarmControls`, `firingHeld` and `bombHeld` are seat 1's. Each seat has its own fire rate law (`SwarmFire` per seat). Two seats start `SEAT_START_OFFSET` (1.5) either side of the bounds center on x (`seatStarts`). `src/game/seats.js` holds the helpers over the seat list (`liveSeats`, `seekTargets`, `pickupAnchor`, `partnerFor`, `framedFocuses`) and the game-side seat work that takes the `Game` (`uploadSwarms`, `fireSeats`, `applySwarmHits`, `reviveSeats`); a seat is live while it is not down and has ships.
- **Joining**: only at the title, by pad. `SeatJoin` (`src/input/seat-join.js`, pure, polled by `createCoopJoin` in `src/ui/coop-join.js` while the title shows) seats a pad on a fresh press: on a pad that is not the first connected one any of standard buttons 0 to 7 (`SECOND_PAD_JOIN_BUTTON_INDICES`), on the first connected pad only a shoulder or trigger, buttons 4 to 7 (`JOIN_BUTTON_INDICES`), since its face buttons start the solo run. A shoulder or trigger press on seat 2's pad leaves. Buttons held when the title opens, or at the first poll, count as held; a browser lists a pad only after its first press, so an unseated pad that first appears with a join button down joins at once, while seat 2's own pad appearing is primed (no leave press). The press that joins is not also a menu press (`menuInput.setExtraPad` re-reads the held buttons). A toast says `Player 2 joined` or `Player 2 left`; `createJoinBanner` (`src/ui/join-banner.js`, `.join-banner` in `menu.css`, in seat 2's color, out of flow at the top of the title menu) shows how to join or that seat 2 joined. Starting calls `Game.setPlayerCount(coopJoin.playerCount)`; there is no mid-run joining. The lab is solo: `openLab` resets the join.
- **Devices**: seat 1 reads keyboard, mouse, touch and the first connected pad that is not seat 2's (`readGamepad` with `excludeIndex`, `input.state`); seat 2 reads its one pad by `Gamepad.index` (`readGamepadAt`, `input.seatTwoState`), so no pad drives both seats. Menus read the first pad and, once joined, seat 2's. The first gesture of either seat starts or restarts the run. Seat 2's pad disconnecting mid-run pauses under the pause menu with the toast `Player 2 controller disconnected`; `coopJoin.handleConnect` gives the seat to that pad returning under any index (toast `Player 2 controller reconnected`). Rumble goes to the pad of the seat concerned (`withHaptics` `padForSeat`; `rumble` takes `undefined` for the first pad with an actuator, an index, or `null` for none).
- **Down and revive**: a seat that loses its last ship while another still has ships goes down (`seatGoesDown`): it drops its weapons and turrets, the multiplier resets (`score.recordHit`, the solo death rule), a strength 1 bloom shockwave plays at its last ship, its pad rumbles, and a running time warp is left alone. A down seat does not move, fire, collect or bomb, is not drawn and is not framed. `SeatRevival` (`src/game/seat-revival.js`) counts scored kills for each down seat; at `REVIVE_KILLS` (100) the seat returns with `REVIVE_SHIPS` (12) ships at `REVIVE_OFFSET` (0.6) beside the first live seat's point (`revivePoint`: the other side at a wall, inside the world bounds), with `REVIVE_INVULNERABLE_SECONDS` (2) of contact immunity for that seat alone and a 0.5 shockwave. When no seat is left live the run's one death follows (death clear, then game over); two seats wiped in one stats frame are that death, not a down and a revive, and the death flood is at the last-wiped seat's point (seat 2's when both fell in one frame).
- **Shared score, bombs and awards**: one `ScoreKeeper`, bomb pool and `Awards`; every scored kill grows the swarm of every seat that is not down (`PlayerSwarm.addKills`; a down seat gets no growth, and `setInitialSwarmShips` skips it). The bomb press of either seat that is not down spends the pool, centered on that seat's swarm with the unzoomed view's radius (`bombRadiusForView`), so zooming out never makes a bomb a world clear.
- **Pickups**: one pickup rotation; the spawn anchor is the midpoint of the live seats' points (`pickupAnchor`) and the base-gun recovery guard waits for every live seat to be on the base gun. The seat whose point or ship reaches a pickup collects it (its own `WeaponState` and turrets); a time warp stays global. At most one `weaponFire` cue plays per update, seat 1 first.
- **Neutral swarm**: `Simulation.setPlayerState` carries the first live seat's point (seat 1's when none is live) and `setSecondPlayer` and `clearSecondPlayer` the second live seat's, in `SimParams` bytes 68 to 79 (the active flag at 68, the position at 72); `steer.wgsl` seeks the nearer of the two, `spawn.wgsl` keeps spawns `nearestPlayerDistance` from both, and `SpawnDirector` takes the second live seat as `partner` (the safe radius and the aim lane apply to either player). Each seat's beam has its own projectile slot (`Projectiles.setBeamSlotCount`, `setBeam(beam, seat)`).
- **Camera**: with two live seats the camera centers on the bounding box of their camera focuses (`framingCenter`) and targets `fitZoom`: `min(1, viewWidth / spanX, viewHeight / spanY)` over the span of the focuses plus `CAMERA_FIT_MARGIN` (2.5) world units on each side, never below `MINIMUM_CAMERA_ZOOM` (0.5). A down seat leaves the frame (seat 1's focus alone when none is live). The trail decay resamples around a zoom change (`trailShiftFor`), and the swarm ship size follows the glyphs' square-root law of the zoom. The simulation never reads the camera.
- **Colors and HUD**: `SEAT_COLORS` (`src/game/seat-colors.js`): seat 1 white, seat 2 green (body `[0.45, 1, 0.55]`, CSS `hsl(130, 90%, 65%)`); `src/render/swarm.js` draws one swarm per seat (`Game.scene().swarms`, each with its `bodyColor`; `scene.swarm` is the first). Projectiles stay untinted. `Game.state.seats` has one entry per seat (`ships`, `armed`, `tier`, `charge`, `railTurrets`, `railTier`, `down`, `reviveKills`, `reviveKillsNeeded`); every other `state` field is seat 1's. The HUD shows two seat rows in the seats' colors (`src/ui/hud-seat.js`, for example `P1 ▲ 12 BASE` and `P2 DOWN 40/100`) under the icons, for a whole two-player run only.

## Audio and probe rules

- Audio: notes are scheduled at least 100 ms ahead against `AudioContext.currentTime` through `StepSequencer` (lookahead 0.1 s, a timer only advances the schedule); never `setTimeout`-driven notes. The gesture that starts the run is the single unlock (`audio.unlock()` inside the event handler; a gamepad press is not an activation). Music tempo is 124 to 134 bpm, changed on bar starts only. Tests fake the audio context and inject time.
- Probe contract: the frame-time probe (`FrameTimeProbe` through `QualityController`) runs during Playing only (`Game.acceptsProbeFrames`), target 10 ms, at most one step per 3 s; it steps down particles, then bloom, then capacity (a preset down). `?probe=off` or `?capacity=` disables it; every browser check passes `probe=off`. Presets change counts and budgets only, never gameplay.

## Pass order

Each animation frame `FrameLoop` runs zero or more fixed update steps, then one render. Each is its own queue submission.

- Update (`Simulation.step`): spawn (in the discs of the step's emitting spawn gates, overflowing outward where the previous step's coarse field is crowded; in the wall band, with the density bias, when no gate emits), grid count, grid scan, grid scatter, coarse field downsample, coarse field blur, density readback (every 15th step, `src/sim/density-readback.js`), steer, integrate, kill-all (count, then kill; world-wide only in the step after `requestKillAll`, the run start and the death clear, or radial after `requestRadialKill`, the bomb around the swarm and the pickup blast around the ship: a `KillArea` uniform at binding 6 limits it to agents within a radius; of two radial requests in one step the larger radius wins, and a kill-all in the same step wins over both; kills are unscored), registered passes (`Simulation.registerPass`; `Projectiles.encode` runs projectile step, collide, rings and beam here), player contact with the hit latch (once `setPlayerState` has been called; the contact uniform holds up to 512 slots, one 256-slot half per seat, bit k of `armedSeats` arms seat k, and `seatCount` seats' workgroups are dispatched), debug kill (only while armed; only the browser checks arm it), particle step (recounts the pool's live particles), stats, stats copy into the staging ring. Every kill asks for `killBurstParticles` (12) sparks plus `killFragmentParticles` (3) heavy fragments from one per-step budget, the pool slots free after the last particle step; kill-all thins its bursts evenly when a clear would outrun it and dims them to a light budget, and in the death clear's step it first emits the death flood (a white flash and `deathFloodParticles` at the ship). The step clears the pool's per-step claims and only the stats buffer's per-frame prefix; its persistent tail (`playerHitSequence`, `hitLatch`, the cumulative scored `kills` counter and both swarm hit masks) survives (the stats buffer is `STATS_WORD_COUNT` = 48 words: the first mask at words 24 to 31, the second at 32 to 39, words 40 to 47 spare), and a capacity swap (`commitCapacity`, its own submission between two frames) copies it into the new simulation along with the compacted alive agents, the second seek point and the seats' contact tables, then builds the new simulation's grid and coarse field so the next render can draw it. The projectile step counts pulse ring detonations into the prefix (`ringsThisFrame`, `lastRingX`, `lastRingY`).
- Render (`Renderer.render`, around the camera that `main.js` sets with `Viewport.setCamera(game.camera.sample(alpha))` before each render): warp grid layer (the whole world's lattice, then the world border: `WorldBorder`, `src/render/world-border.js` and `worldborder.wgsl`, one quad over the world rectangle whose fragment shader lights the outline as a whole: a square-cornered Chebyshev core of `BORDER_CORE_HALF_PIXELS` plus a glow of `BORDER_GLOW_PIXELS` (Euclidean, so it rounds outside corners), both device pixels scaled by the device pixel ratio, in the same cleared layer so the bloom lights it and it cannot smear; nothing is drawn outside the world; `WORLD_WARP_LATTICE` at the one-screen spacing, or `PHONE_WARP_LATTICE` with the one-screen line count on a phone; with this frame's shockwave impulses), trails decay (reading the previous scene shifted by the camera's movement since the last frame in whole device pixels, `trailOriginFor`), spawn gates (`Simulation.spawnGates`), glyphs (extrapolated from the newest step, or at half simulation rate interpolated from `previousAgentsBuffer`), projectiles, pickups, particles, ship and charge ring, bloom, composite. `Game.scene()` supplies the projectiles, pickups and ship; the ship hides from death until respawn, and at game over. After it `main.js` updates the HUD and the edge indicators (`src/ui/edgeIndicators.js`: DOM chevrons on the view edge for off-screen pickups and the telegraphed wave gate).

## Frame order of operations

One `Game.update(deltaSeconds)` is one fixed step (full comments at the top of `src/game/game.js`):

Steps 1 to 4 and 6 run per seat in seat order (`Game.seats`: one in solo; seat 1 reads `input.state`, seat 2 `input.seatTwoState`).

1. Input: `input.update`; in Ready, the first gesture (fire, bomb, movement or stick) of either seat starts the run, and a start clears the world with one kill-all. A rising bomb edge of a seat that is not down, accepted by `RunState.useBomb` (one shared pool), requests a radial kill before this step, centered on that seat's swarm centroid with a radius of 1.2 view half-diagonals (`bombRadiusForView` in `src/game/camera.js`).
2. `PlayerShip.update` (Playing only); the mouse aims from the ship to the pointer unless a stick or touch aim is pushed; with the beam armed the aim turns at most `beamTurnRevolutionsPerSecond` (default 1.5, lab knob, 0 is instant) via `turnAimToward`. A down seat does not move. After the swarms move, `Game.followCamera` updates the camera toward `framingCenter` of the live seats' `cameraFocus` values (each half the seat's controlled point, half its swarm centroid) and its zoom toward `fitZoom` (1 with one live seat); a start, a quit and a respawn cut it there, and watch mode holds it still.
3. `PickupDirector.update` (anchored on `pickupAnchor`, with `densityAt`: a spawn takes the lowest-density of up to 8 candidates on the placement annulus, 3 to 5 units for the 16:9 view and scaled by the view half-diagonal, inside the world and a view-sized rectangle around the player), then collection at each seat that is not down (its point, else its ships) into that seat's `WeaponState.collect`, before charge expiry; a rail pickup adds a rear-firing turret (`railTurrets`, up to `MAXIMUM_RAIL_TURRETS` = 12, 30 degrees apart around the rear of the aim; later pickups raise `railTier`) and leaves the armed weapon, its tier and charge alone (owner ruling R29). A collection also plays the pickup effect and, with `Game.pickupBlastRadius` above 0 (default 1.5, `setPickupBlastRadius`), calls `Simulation.requestRadialKill` at the ship and queues a 0.5 warp impulse; radius 0 skips the blast and queues the 0.15 impulse. A time warp pickup bypasses `WeaponState.collect`: it starts or extends the warp (`TimeWarp.start`), plays `pickup('timewarp')` and queues a 0.6 impulse in place of the 0.5 or 0.15 one (the blast still fires). The recovery guard's pity spawns always offer a weapon: on the time warp slot they take the next slot's weapon.
4. For each seat that is not down, `WeaponState.update` (armed kind, tier, charge and cue cadence; its own volley spawns are not queued), then `SwarmFire.distribute`: the ships' per-ship shots, then the turrets' rail spawns (`turretSpawns`, at the rail interval while firing, beside any weapon including the beam), go to `Projectiles.queueSpawn` and the beam to `setBeam` (its own slot per seat with two seats; cleared on the expiry frame, which keeps the turrets). One `weaponFire` cue per firing frame: the armed weapon's, or `'rail'` at the turret tier when only the turrets fired.
5. `Simulation.setPlayerState` with the first live seat's point (`setPlayer` only in Ready, so contact stays off until start; with two seats also `setSecondPlayer` or `clearSecondPlayer`), then the spawn director (`SpawnDirector.update` with the camera's view: gates open on a ring 1 to 1.4 view half-diagonals from the view center, just outside the view and inside the world, drift, and close, also when left more than 2 half-diagonals behind; one wave at a time from a gate outside the safe radius and the aim lane; no waves in Ready, where every gate trickles) and spawn pressure (`SpawnPressure.update` with the latest stats, the step's sequence, so spawns still in flight are not requested twice, and the director's wave, so refills bank between waves and pour out during them; both paused while dying and respawning, when the step gets no gates) and its ramped seek scale and spawn distance (`setSeekScale`, `setSpawnDistanceFraction`; the ramp restarts on start and respawn and is retuned through `SpawnPressure.setRamp`), then each seat's ships are uploaded for contact (`uploadSwarms`, with its revive immunity) and the simulation step is submitted with the director's gate table (`SpawnDirector.spawnGates`). During a time warp (`TimeWarp`, started by a `timewarp` pickup) the director, spawn pressure and the simulation get the step's delta times `timeWarp.scale` while the projectile step gets the unscaled delta (`SimParams.projectileDt`); the warp timer counts real time, pausing keeps it, and death, a new run and quit to title end it.
6. Every ready stats frame (`readAllStats`, oldest first): kills (`killsSinceLastRead`, the difference of the cumulative counter) score once per stats frame and only while Playing, kills grow every seat's swarm that is not down and count toward each down seat's revive (`reviveSeats`, which brings a seat back beside the first live seat), awards add bombs (lives are dropped), ring detonations queue a shockwave, and each seat's hit mask (`applySwarmHits`) kills its hit ships with a small shockwave each: a seat wiped while another has ships goes down, and when no seat is left `RunState.playerHit` is offered the death (which drops every seat's weapons and turrets, ends a time warp and resets the multiplier).
7. `RunState.update` drains actions: clear the world (the death clear with the death flood, a world-wide kill-all) and projectiles, queue bomb and death shockwaves, respawn.

Watch mode (the lab; `Game.setWatch(fraction)`, `null` leaves it) keeps the phase Ready and disables the gesture start, so the Playing-only work of steps 2 to 4 and 6 (player movement, pickup collection, fire, scoring) never runs and contact stays off (`setPlayer` only); step 2 instead moves the player point to follow the pointer (or the move stick) so the swarm chases it, and step 5 runs a watch `SpawnPressure` with full seek that holds the alive target at `fraction` of capacity, with the director in continuous mode (no waves). `Game.start()` (the lab's Play) leaves watch mode too, and leaving the lab restores the state captured on entry (`captureLabState`).

The loading screen (`src/ui/loading.js`) covers boot and the warm-up: the first rendered frames, until at least 30 have run with the latest 10 each under 34 ms, or 3 s have passed (`WarmUp`, fed `deltaSeconds`). Until it finishes the run cannot start (`startsOnGesture` stays false, polled menu actions are dropped) and `window.__murmuration.ready` stays false.

While paused (`Game.pause`, from Escape, the HUD pause button, gamepad Options, blur, a hidden tab or the rotate overlay; only from Playing, Dying or Respawning), `Game.update` returns at once: no input read, no step, no stats read, no timers, so scores, lives and every sequence guard are exactly as they were and a hit in flight is handled after `resume()` by the usual rules; `state.phase` reads `paused`, a bomb or fire held through the pause needs a fresh press, and the main loop skips `audio.update` and ducks the music (`setPaused`). The title menu sets `Game.startsOnGesture = false` while it shows, so only its own start (button, arena tap, Enter, Space, face button) starts the run; `Game.quitToTitle` returns to Ready.

A capacity change (`Game.setCapacity` through `CapacitySwap`) is prepared (`Simulation.prepareCapacity`: buffers and pipelines) while the old simulation keeps stepping; commit and install then run synchronously between two updates, so play never pauses and no step is lost. A prepare that has not settled after `timeoutMilliseconds` (10000, on a monotonic clock) is ended by `CapacitySwap.expire`, which `Game.render` polls every frame: the request rejects with an Error named `CapacitySwapTimeout`, the pending flag clears so the probe and later swaps work again, and a prepare that resolves late is discarded. `settleApply` in `src/game/quality.js` applies a preset only when the swap succeeded. At half simulation rate (`Game.setSimulationRate('half')`, the `phoneLow` preset) the spawn pressure and simulation step of step 5 run every other update with both updates' delta; everything else still runs every update. `QualityController` (`src/game/quality.js`) takes frame times from `src/gpu/frame-timer.js` while Playing and applies presets between frames. After each update `main.js` hands `Game.freshStats` (the newest frame read this update, kills summed), the run phase and the multiplier to the audio hook (`IntensityTracker`: survival time, multiplier and kill rate); the game plays its cues itself through the hook's `cues` (`Game.create({ effects })`: bomb, death, pickup, expiry and `weaponFire(kind, tier)`, each an effect plus the matching music cue). Audio unlocks inside the gesture that starts the run (canvas pointerdown or touchstart, a key, or `audio.unlock()` from a button).

Event order inside the GPU step follows the pass order above: bomb kill-all or pickup blast, projectile kills, player contact. A pickup collected in the frame of charge expiry wins. A sidewinder detonates on its first kill into a half-size pulse ring and a pulse orb on its first kill into a full one (`projectiles_collide.wgsl`; rings pierce; an orb that meets nothing bursts at the end of its flight, at the arena edge or in a coarse cell denser than its `extra`); every other projectile kind pierces. GPU events carry `frameSequence`; the CPU ignores hits that predate the current respawn.
