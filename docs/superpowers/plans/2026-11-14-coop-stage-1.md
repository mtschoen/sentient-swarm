# Shared-Screen Co-op, Stage 1 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Two people on one screen each fly one player swarm against the neutral GPU swarm, with press-to-join at the title, a shared score, down-and-revive lives and a camera that zooms out to fit both, while the solo game stays exactly as it is.

**Architecture:** A `PlayerSeat` object holds one player's `PlayerShip`, `PlayerSwarm`, `SwarmFire`, `WeaponState` and held-button state; `Game` loops over one or two seats. The GPU side gains a second seek point in the spare SimParams bytes 68 to 79 (the neutral swarm seeks, and spawns keep their distance from, the nearest live player), one 512-slot contact uniform with a per-seat armed bit, and a second persistent hit mask in stats words 32 to 39. Input becomes per pad (a device registry keyed by `Gamepad.index`), the follow camera gains a zoom, and the renderer draws one swarm per seat in the seat's color.

**Tech Stack:** Plain JavaScript ES modules, WGSL, WebGPU, `node:test`, Playwright (dev only), Vite (dev only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-multiplayer-design.md` (sections 2, 3.4, 4.1, 4.2, 6 stage 1, 8), plus the owner rulings and decisions below, which win over the spec where they differ.

### Owner rulings (binding, verbatim)

- Join flow: "Press to join at title": at the title, a second gamepad pressing a button claims seat 2 (shown with its color); starting begins a two-player run; nobody joins means solo exactly as today; no mid-run joining in stage 1.
- Lives: "Down, revived on kills": a wiped player is out until the survivor scores 100 more kills, then respawns with 12 ships next to the survivor; the run ends when both are wiped at once; shared score.
- Camera: "Zoom out to fit both": centered between the swarms, zooming out smoothly as they separate, down to the whole world at most (zoom 0.5, the world is two views wide and high); no wall between players.
- Swarm feel stays the baked "bees" motion per player. Do not touch spawn patterns. Do not change firepower numbers or the beam's rules.

### Decisions (reversible; each line is the choice taken)

- Fire rate: the existing 0.4-exponent law applies per seat independently (each seat has its own `SwarmFire`).
- Pickups: the collecting seat gets the weapon; each seat has its own `WeaponState` and turrets; time warp stays global.
- Devices: seat 1 is keyboard, mouse, touch and every pad not held by seat 2; seat 2 is one pad. Two pads also work. Nobody joined: seat 1 reads exactly as today (first connected pad).
- Join press: a fresh press of a shoulder or trigger (standard buttons 4, 5, 6, 7, `JOIN_BUTTON_INDICES`) on any pad at the title claims seat 2, and the same press on that pad leaves. These buttons do nothing at the title today, so solo starting (face buttons, Enter, Space, a tap) is untouched.
- Colors: seat 1 white; seat 2 green, `SEAT_COLORS[1]` body `[0.45, 1, 0.55]`, CSS `hsl(130, 90%, 65%)` (away from Chaser red 0.02, Skittish 0.15, Orbiter 0.55, Predator 0.92). Projectiles stay untinted.
- Bombs and awards: one shared pool; either seat's press spends it, centered on that seat's swarm, with the solo radius (the unzoomed view), so zooming out never turns a bomb into a world clear.
- Kills: shared score; every live seat's swarm grows from every scored kill (no projectile owner exists in stage 1).
- A seat going down drops its weapons and turrets, resets the multiplier (the solo death rule), queues a strength 1 bloom shockwave at its last ship and leaves a running time warp alone.
- Revive: `REVIVE_SHIPS = 12` ships at `REVIVE_OFFSET = 0.6` world units beside the survivor's point, with `REVIVE_INVULNERABLE_SECONDS = 2` of contact immunity for that seat (the solo respawn's 2 s).
- Seat count: exactly 2 (`MAXIMUM_PLAYER_SEATS = 2`); nothing generalizes to 4.
- Camera: `MINIMUM_CAMERA_ZOOM = 0.5`, `CAMERA_ZOOM_SMOOTH_SECONDS = 0.5`, `CAMERA_FIT_MARGIN = 2.5` world units around the seats' camera focuses; a downed seat leaves the frame.
- Audio: at most one `weaponFire` cue per update (seat 1 first); a seat down rumbles that seat's pad and plays no new sound.
- Lab and watch mode are solo only: `setWatch` and `quitToTitle` return to one seat.

## Global Constraints

- Plain JavaScript ES modules and WGSL. No TypeScript. No runtime dependencies; Vite and Playwright are dev dependencies only.
- Full-word identifiers (`maximum`, not `max`; `position`, not `pos`) in JavaScript and in WGSL user identifiers.
- No em-dashes (U+2014) anywhere, including comments, docs and commit messages.
- Every source file under about 500 lines, or carrying a one-line note at the top saying why. `src/game/game.js` (770 lines today, with its note) must not end any task longer than 770 lines.
- No hard-coded machine paths. No wall-clock assertions in tests; inject time.
- Mobile WebGPU limits: at most 8 storage buffers per shader stage; 16-byte aligned structs, no `vec3` in storage layouts; `@workgroup_size` at most 256; workgroup memory at most 16 KB.
- Neighbor contributions are averaged, never summed; `MAXIMUM_NEIGHBOR_SAMPLES = 32` stays.
- Shaders load through `fetch(new URL('./shaders/<name>.wgsl', import.meta.url))`.
- Commit after every task with a message in the imperative, ending with the trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Every task leaves `npm test`, `npm run coverage` (lines 92, functions 86) and `npm run build` green; tasks that touch WGSL or GPU wiring also run `npm run check:gpu`; tasks that touch the page run `npm run smoke`. Solo behavior is unchanged after every task.

## Review Focus

1. Seat 2's pad unplugs mid-run, then comes back with a different `Gamepad.index`: the run pauses under the pause menu with a toast, and the returning pad reclaims seat 2 (tests in Task 8 and Task 13).
2. Both seats wiped in the same stats frame: the run ends (death clear, game over); it is not one seat down and a revive (test in Task 12).
3. Stale hit frames: stats frames from before a revive or from a reused slot never kill revived ships, and a capacity swap mid co-op run keeps the second hit mask and the second seek point (tests in Tasks 2, 3 and 12).
4. One pad plus keyboard, nobody joined: seat 1 still reads that pad exactly as today; once that pad joins seat 2, seat 1 stops reading it, so no pad drives both seats (test in Task 7).
5. The survivor stands against a wall when the revive comes: the revived seat appears inside the world bounds, beside the survivor (test in Task 12).

## Risks and open points

- **Join button.** "A second gamepad pressing a button" is read as a fresh press of a shoulder or trigger (buttons 4 to 7), because every face button already starts the solo run at the title. If the owner wants literally any button on a pad other than the one navigating, `SeatJoin` needs a notion of the menu pad; a one-line change to `JOIN_BUTTON_INDICES` covers other button choices.
- **Solo output of the shader edits.** Tasks 3 and 6 edit `steer.wgsl`, `spawn.wgsl`, `glyphs.wgsl`, `swarm.wgsl`, `trails_decay.wgsl` and `player_collide.wgsl`. Each solo path returns the old expression behind a uniform branch, but WGSL lets the compiler reassociate and fuse float operations, so solo output could change in the last bit; the steer pass's frame time with the extra branch is not measured. The `render-capture.mjs` comparison in Tasks 3 and 6 is the evidence; the neutral swarm is not reproducible run to run anyway (atomic grid order), so only rendering is compared.
- **Spawn look in co-op.** At zoom 0.5 the view is as large as the world, and the director's existing rule then places gates in the wall band instead of the view ring. No spawn code changes, but co-op waves will look different from solo ones. Task 9 extends only the safety distances and aim lane to the second player, which the scope asks for.
- **Trails under zoom** resample with `floor` of scaled coordinates, so a zoom change blurs a frame's history slightly; solo never takes that path.
- **Drawn size versus hitbox.** Task 6 enlarges the swarm darts by `zoom ** -0.5` when zoomed out, so at zoom 0.5 a ship is drawn about 1.4 times its contact radius.
- **Growth doubles in co-op.** Every live swarm grows from every shared kill (decision above); with two per-seat fire laws this is generous and untested for balance.
- **Menus read every pad** after Task 7: with two pads and nobody joined, the second pad can now navigate and pause (today it is ignored). Gameplay still reads the first pad only.
- **Edge chevrons** use `visibleBounds`, which includes the lab's inspect zoom, so lab chevrons move while inspecting (they were wrong there before).
- **Readability** of the green swarm under bloom against half a million glyphs (spec section 8) is untested; Task 13's screenshot is for the owner's taste pass, and the owner's attended run is the last stage.
- **Not measured or verified:** two CPU flocks per step on a phone; gamepad index reuse on reconnect outside Chromium (MDN pages disagree); `vibrationActuator` per pad on Firefox and Safari; the timing of run C in `coop.mjs` (its frame limits are guesses modeled on `swarm-spike.mjs`).
- **Calls I made that the owner may reverse:** 2 s revive immunity, the multiplier reset on a seat down, the revive offset, the fit margin and zoom smoothing, seat 2's color, and that a seat down leaves the time warp running.
